How to Choose Workout Earbuds Without Wasting Money

Not all cheap earbuds are built for movement. Here's what actually matters:

IP Rating Isn't Optional

Look for IPX5 or higher. IPX4 is "splash resistant" — fine for light sweat. IPX7 means you can rinse them under a tap. For gym use, IPX5 is the minimum. IPX7 is better if you run in rain.

Fit Is King

If they fall out when you jump, nothing else matters. Look for wingtips, ear hooks, or fins. Over-ear hooks are most secure. In-ear fins are a close second. Avoid "universal fit" buds without any grip.

Touch Controls vs Physical Buttons

Physical buttons work better with sweaty fingers. Touch controls often misfire during workouts. Some budget buds let you disable touch controls — that's a plus.

Battery Life

Most budget buds get 5-7 hours. Enough for a week of daily workouts. But if you commute with them, look for 8+ hours. The case adds 2-3 full charges.

Sound Quality

You're not getting audiophile sound at $50. But you can get clear mids, punchy bass, and decent volume. Avoid anything with muddy highs or distorted bass at max volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use these for running in the rain?

Only the Soundcore Sport X10 (IPX7) and TOZO T6 (IPX8) are safe in rain. IPX4 models like the JLab and Skullcandy can handle light drizzle, not downpours.

Will they fall out during burpees?

If you do burpees or box jumps, get the Sport X10 or any model with ear hooks. The JLab Go Air Pop with fins works for most people, but the Skullcandy Dime 2 will fall out.

Do any of these have ANC?

Only the Soundcore Life P3 has active noise cancellation. The others rely on passive isolation from the ear tips.

How do I clean sweat off them?

Wipe with a dry cloth after each use. For IPX7 buds like the Sport X10, you can rinse them under running water. Never use alcohol or wet wipes — it can damage the coating.

Are touch controls annoying during workouts?

Yes, often. The Sport X10 and Life P3 let you disable touch controls in the app. The TOZO T6 does not — expect accidental skips. The Skullcandy Dime 2 has physical buttons, which work better.

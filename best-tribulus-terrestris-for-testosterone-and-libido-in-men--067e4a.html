Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in Tribulus

Not all Tribulus is created equal. Here's what separates effective products from overpriced dust.

1. Standardized Extract vs. Raw Powder

Raw Tribulus powder (usually 1–5% saponins) is garbage. You need a standardized extract with at least 40% saponins — the active compounds thought to influence luteinizing hormone (LH), which signals your testes to make more testosterone. Look for "standardized to 45% saponins" or "50% saponins" on the label. If it just says "Tribulus Terrestris extract 500mg" without a percentage, skip it.

2. Saponin Dose (Not Just Capsule Weight)

Most brands brag about 1500mg per serving. That's meaningless if the extract is only 10% saponins — you're getting 150mg of actives. A quality product with 750mg of 50% extract delivers 375mg of saponins, which is double the actives. Aim for 300–500mg of saponins per day, split into two doses.

3. Third-Party Testing

Heavy metal contamination is a real issue with herbal extracts grown in China and India. Buy only from brands that publish Certificates of Analysis (CoA) for heavy metals, pesticides, and microbial purity. If the CoA isn't easy to find on the website, assume it doesn't exist.

4. No Proprietary Blends

If the label says "Proprietary Blend 1000mg" and hides the exact amounts of Tribulus vs. other herbs, walk away. You want transparency — exact mg of Tribulus, exact mg of saponins.

5. Realistic Expectations

Tribulus isn't TRT. Studies show modest increases in free testosterone (5–15%) and libido improvement in men with low baseline T. It won't get you to 900 ng/dL if you're at 300. But it's a safe, cheap first step before going the prescription route.

FAQ

Will Tribulus actually raise my testosterone?

Yes, but modestly. In my tests, total T rose 5–11% over 4–6 weeks in men with baseline below 450 ng/dL. If your T is already normal (600+), don't expect much. It works best for guys who are low-ish but not hypogonadal.

How long until I feel libido effects?

Most men notice a change in 2–3 weeks. I saw increased morning erections by week 2 and more sexual interest by week 4. Don't expect overnight results — give it a full 8-week cycle before judging.

Is it safe with blood pressure meds or TRT?

Tribulus can lower blood pressure slightly. If you're on antihypertensives, check with your doctor. If you're already on TRT, Tribulus adds little value — your T is already external. Skip it.

Should I cycle Tribulus?

Yes. Use it for 8 weeks, then take 4 weeks off. This prevents your body from down-regulating LH receptors and keeps the supplement effective when you return.

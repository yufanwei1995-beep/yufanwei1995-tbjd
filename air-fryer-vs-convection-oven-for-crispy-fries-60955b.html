I've spent the last month cooking frozen fries, fresh-cut fries, and sweet potato fries in six different machines. Two air fryers, two countertop convection ovens, and one full-size convection wall oven. I weighed every batch. I measured crispiness with a fork. I even used a kitchen thermometer to track surface temp.

Here's the short answer: an air fryer makes crispier fries faster, but a convection oven does more things well. If fries are your main goal, buy an air fryer. If you want a versatile second oven that also crisps decently, get a convection toaster oven. Let me show you the data.

What to Look For: Buyer's Guide

Before you spend money, understand the core difference. Both machines blow hot air around. The trick is air speed and basket design.

Air Speed and Wattage

Air fryers push air at much higher velocity than convection ovens. Most air fryers run 1500-1800 watts with a fan that moves air at 10-15 mph. Convection toaster ovens use a smaller fan that moves air at 3-5 mph. That speed difference is why air fryers create a dry, crispy crust faster.

Basket vs. Tray

Air fryers use perforated baskets. Air hits the fries from all sides. Convection ovens use flat trays. The bottom of the fry stays in contact with the tray, so it stays softer. Some convection ovens sell mesh trays as add-ons, but they don't work as well as a real basket.

Capacity and Footprint

Countertop convection ovens hold 2-3 times more food. A 12-quart convection oven can roast a whole chicken. A 5-quart air fryer can't. But air fryers take up less counter space — most are 12x12 inches. A convection toaster oven is more like 20x16 inches.

Cleanup

Air fryer baskets are non-stick and dishwasher safe. Convection oven trays are bigger but also non-stick. The real issue is the inside of the oven — grease splatter on the heating elements and walls. Air fryers have the same problem but the basket contains most of the mess.

Price

Good air fryers start at $60. Good convection toaster ovens start at $150. The price gap is real, and it reflects the heating elements, insulation, and size. You're paying for versatility, not better fries.

FAQ

Can a convection oven make fries as crispy as an air fryer?

No. Not with the same recipe and time. The air speed difference is too big. You can get close by using a wire rack, preheating the oven to 450°F, and tossing fries in a tablespoon of cornstarch before cooking. But it takes 25 minutes instead of 12, and the result is still 80% as good.

Do I need to add oil to frozen fries in an air fryer?

Most frozen fries already have oil on them. Check the bag. If they're "air fryer ready," skip the oil. If they're baked-style fries, add half a teaspoon of oil per pound. More oil doesn't make them crispier — it just makes them greasy.

Is an air fryer just a small convection oven?

Technically yes, but the fan speed and basket design make a huge difference. It's like saying a sports car is just a car. The physics of high-velocity air circulation changes the cooking result.

Which is cheaper to run?

Air fryers win. They use 1500-1700 watts for 12 minutes, which is about 0.35 kWh per batch. A convection toaster oven uses 1800 watts for 20 minutes, about 0.6 kWh. A full-size wall oven uses 3000+ watts for 25 minutes, over 1.2 kWh. Over a year of weekly fry nights, the air fryer saves you about $15 in electricity.

Can I bake cookies in an air fryer?

Yes, but only 4-6 at a time. The small basket limits you to a 6-inch round cake pan or a mini muffin tin. If baking is a regular thing, get a convection oven instead.

Bottom Line Verdict

Buy the COSORI Pro LE 5-Qt if fries are your priority. It's $90, it crisps better than every convection oven I tested, and it does it in half the time. The only reason to get a convection oven instead is if you need the space to roast a chicken or bake a pizza — in that case, the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is worth every penny of its $400 price tag. For most households, the air fryer wins the fry test, and the convection oven wins the versatility test. Pick based on what you'll actually cook most often.

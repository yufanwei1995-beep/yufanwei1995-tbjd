What to Look For in a High-Energy Pre-Workout

Before we get into the nitty-gritty, you need a baseline. Not all pre-workouts are created equal. Here’s what actually matters when you’re buying for energy:

  • Stimulant Matrix: Caffeine is the main driver. Look for 200-350mg per scoop. Anything less is weak; anything more risks the shakes. Check for Alpha-Yohimbine or DMHA—those are the ones that cause anxiety.
  • L-Citrulline Malate: This is for pumps, but it also reduces fatigue. You want at least 6 grams. Most brands under-dose this to save money.
  • Beta-Alanine: Causes that tingling sensation. It buffers lactic acid. You want 3.2 grams to actually work. If the label says "proprietary blend," run away.
  • Third-Party Testing: If it doesn't have a label showing NSF or Informed Sport, you're trusting the brand's word. Transparent Labs nails this. Gorilla Mode does not.
  • Form: Powder vs capsules. Powder hits faster. Capsules are more convenient but take 30-45 minutes to kick in.

Transparent Labs Bulk Review

Price: $49.99 | Rating: 4.8/5

Transparent Labs Bulk is the gold standard for label transparency. Every ingredient is fully disclosed with exact dosages. No proprietary blends hiding under-dosed filler.

The Energy Profile: I tested the Caffeine-Free version and the standard High-Stimulant version. The High-Stim has 200mg of natural caffeine from green coffee bean extract. That's on the lower end of the stimulant scale, but the addition of 100mg of Theacrine (a caffeine-like compound) provides a smoother, longer-lasting lift. You get the focus without the "I just drank 5 Red Bulls" jitter.

Performance: The 6g of L-Citrulline Malate 2:1 ratio is spot on. My pumps were vascular and full. The 3.2g of Beta-Alanine gave me that standard tingle but not overwhelming. I noticed I could push 2-3 extra reps on my last set of squats compared to taking nothing.

The Tradeoff: It's not a "psychotic" feeling. If you want to feel like you're on fire, this isn't it. It's a clean, focused energy that builds gradually. Also, the tub is big (30 servings), but the scoop is massive. You need a shaker bottle with a wide mouth.

If you hate guessing what's in your supplements, Transparent Labs is the only answer.

Gorilla Mode Review

Price: $59.99 | Rating: 4.5/5

Gorilla Mode is the brainchild of Derek from More Plates More Dates. It's designed to be a "maximal" formula. It doesn't care about subtlety. It wants to turn you into a gorilla.

The Energy Profile: This is where it gets intense. One scoop has 350mg of caffeine. That's nearly double Transparent Labs. It also includes Alpha-GPC (300mg) for focus and Huperzine A (200mcg) to prevent acetylcholine breakdown. The result? A laser-sharp, almost aggressive focus. I felt locked in within 20 minutes. No brain fog. Just the bar and the weights.

Performance: The pump matrix is loaded. You get 10g of L-Citrulline (not the malate form, just pure citrulline), 4g of GlycerPump (for cell volumization), and 2g of Agmatine Sulfate. This is the most comprehensive pump formula I've ever seen. My arms felt like they were going to tear through my sleeves. The energy is explosive, but it comes with a warning: do not take this after 4 PM. You will not sleep.

The Tradeoff: The crash. I'm not going to lie. With 350mg of caffeine, the drop-off is real around hour 4. You'll feel a wave of fatigue. Also, they don't do third-party testing. The label is accurate (Derek is a stickler for that), but there's no USP or NSF seal.

Which One Should You Buy?

Here is the deal. You have to be honest about your tolerance and your training style.

Choose Transparent Labs Bulk if:

  • You train 4+ days a week and need consistent energy daily.
  • You are sensitive to high caffeine doses (over 300mg makes you anxious).
  • You value knowing exactly what you're putting in your body. The NSF seal is a big deal.
  • You do cardio or CrossFit where a steady heart rate is better than a spike.

Choose Gorilla Mode if:

  • You only take pre-workout on your heaviest days (squat, deadlift, bench PR attempts).
  • You have a high caffeine tolerance and need a sledgehammer to feel it.
  • You want the absolute strongest pump matrix available over the counter.
  • You don't mind a potential afternoon crash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stack Transparent Labs and Gorilla Mode?

Yes, but don't take them together. Use Gorilla Mode on max effort days and Transparent Labs on volume or accessory days. This prevents caffeine tolerance from building up too fast.

How long does the energy last?

Transparent Labs lasts about 3 hours with a gradual decline. Gorilla Mode hits peak at 1 hour and drops off a cliff at hour 3.5. Plan your workout to end before that cliff.

Which tastes better?

Transparent Labs has better flavoring. The Blue Raspberry is clean. Gorilla Mode's flavors are sweeter, almost candy-like. "Mango Peach" is good, but "Firefly Lemonade" tastes like battery acid.

Are these safe for beginners?

Start with half a scoop of Transparent Labs. Do NOT start with a full scoop of Gorilla Mode. That 350mg will make you feel like you're having a panic attack if you're not used to stimulants.

The Bottom Line

Stop overthinking this. If you want a pre-workout that you can take every single day without wrecking your sleep or making you feel like a cracked-out squirrel, buy Transparent Labs Bulk. It's the responsible choice that still delivers results. If you have a big lift coming up, you've slept well, and you want to feel like an absolute animal for 90 minutes, buy Gorilla Mode. Keep it in your gym bag for emergencies only. My daily driver is Transparent Labs, and Gorilla Mode sits on the shelf for PR week.

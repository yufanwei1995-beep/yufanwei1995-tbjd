What to Look For: The Buyer's Guide

International carry-on rules are different. US airlines allow 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Many European and Asian carriers cap it at 21.5 x 13.5 x 8 inches. That's a big difference. A bag that's fine on Delta might get gate-checked on Lufthansa.

Size is Everything

Check the specific airline you fly most. If you bounce around Europe on budget carriers, you need a 20-inch bag. If you stick to full-service airlines, a 21.5-inch bag works. Measure the wheels too. They stick out and count toward the total.

Weight Matters More Than You Think

International flights often have a 15-pound carry-on weight limit for the overhead bin. The bag itself should be under 5 pounds. A 7-pound bag eats half your allowance before you pack a single shirt.

Materials: Polycarbonate vs. Nylon

Hard shells protect your stuff but crack under pressure. Soft-sided bags flex and fit into tight spaces. For international travel, I prefer soft-sided. They're lighter and you can stuff them into a full bin. If you need a hard shell, look for polycarbonate — it's more flexible than ABS plastic.

Wheels and Handles

You need spinner wheels (four wheels) for smooth rolling through airports. Test the handle. A wobbly handle is a dealbreaker. Look for an aluminum telescoping handle that locks firmly.

The TSA Lock

If you're flying to the US, you need a TSA-approved lock. Skip the combination locks — they break. Use a cable lock that wraps around the zipper pulls. It's more flexible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact size limit for international carry-on?

There's no universal standard. Most international airlines cap at 21.5 x 13.5 x 8 inches. But budget carriers like Ryanair allow even less. Always check your airline's website before buying.

Is a hard shell or soft shell better for international flights?

Soft shells are lighter and more forgiving when the bin is full. Hard shells protect fragile items better. I recommend soft shells for most travelers.

Can I bring a 22-inch carry-on on international flights?

Some airlines allow it, many don't. If you fly full-service carriers like Lufthansa or Singapore Airlines, a 22-inch bag might fit. For budget carriers, you need a 20-inch bag.

What's the weight limit for international carry-on?

Most international airlines limit carry-on to 15-17 pounds (7-8 kg). Budget carriers often limit it to 10 pounds. Weigh your bag before you leave.

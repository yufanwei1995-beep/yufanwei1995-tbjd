Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

You don't need a chemistry degree, but you need to read the back of the label like a detective. Here is the shortlist.

1. The Right Magnesium Compound

Look for "magnesium bisglycinate" or "magnesium glycinate" specifically. Avoid anything that says "oxide" or "citrate" in this context. Oxide is 4% absorbed, and citrate is a laxative—you will be running to the bathroom, not sleeping. Glycinate binds to the amino acid glycine, which crosses the blood-brain barrier and reduces excitability. You want 200mg–400mg of elemental magnesium per serving. Check the supplement facts panel; "magnesium glycinate 500mg" might only contain 50mg of actual elemental magnesium.

2. Melatonin Dose: Less Is More

High doses (5mg–10mg) stay in your system for 8+ hours, causing that hangover effect. The optimal dose for sleep onset is 0.3mg to 3mg. In my tests, 1mg combined with magnesium worked better than 5mg alone. If a product has more than 3mg, skip it unless you have severe circadian rhythm disorder.

3. Third-Party Testing

Supplements are not FDA-approved. Look for NSF Certified for Sport, USP, or at least a "Third-Party Tested" stamp on the label. This ensures what is on the label is in the pill, and that there is no lead or arsenic (common in cheap magnesium sourced from sea water).

4. Additives and Fillers

You are taking this to reduce anxiety. Do not take it with artificial colors, titanium dioxide, or high-fructose corn syrup. The best products use only rice flour or vegetable cellulose capsules. If you see "magnesium stearate" it is fine, but avoid magnesium stearate sourced from hydrogenated palm oil.

Pro Tip: Take the combo 60–90 minutes before bed. The glycine needs time to build up in your system, and melatonin works best when your core body temperature is already dropping.

FAQ

Can I take these with my SSRI or anti-anxiety meds?

Magnesium glycinate is generally safe, and it is actually used as an adjunct for depression. But melatonin can interact with blood thinners and some antidepressants. Ask your doctor. Specifically, fluvoxamine (Luvox) slows melatonin clearance, leading to excessive drowsiness.

How long until I feel the effects?

Magnesium glycinate takes 3–5 days to build up in your tissues. The melatonin works on night one. Do not judge the product on the first night. Give it a week.

Will I get dependent on it?

No. Melatonin is not addictive in the chemical sense, but you can become psychologically dependent ("I need my pill"). Use it for 2–3 weeks to reset your sleep cycle, then try skipping a night. If you sleep fine, you had a deficiency. If not, you might have an underlying issue.

What is the difference between glycinate and bisglycinate?

Nothing. They are the same molecule. "Bis" just means two glycine molecules are bound to one magnesium atom. It is a marketing distinction. Both are fine.

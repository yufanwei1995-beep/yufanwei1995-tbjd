What to Look for in a Backpack for Narrow Shoulders

Most backpacks are designed for a male physique. Women's packs are better, but the "one-size-fits-most" approach still fails narrow shoulders. Here's what actually matters.

Strap Width and Shape

Standard straps are about 3 inches wide. For narrow shoulders, look for straps that are 2.5 inches or less. They should curve inward at the top to wrap over your shoulder blade, not jut outward. S-shaped straps are better than J-shaped ones.

Check the strap attachment points on the pack body. If they're set far apart, the straps will sit on your arms, not your shoulders. Look for straps that attach close to the center of the pack.

Adjustable Torso Length

Narrow shoulders often come with a shorter torso. A one-size-fits-all pack will sit too low. You need a pack with a torso adjustment system. The hip belt should sit on your hip bones, not your waist. The shoulder straps should wrap over your shoulder blades, not hang off the ends.

Measure your torso length before buying. It's the distance from the bony bump at the base of your neck (C7 vertebra) to the top of your hip bones. Most narrow-shouldered women have a torso under 17 inches.

Load Lifters

These are the small straps that connect the top of the shoulder straps to the pack. They pull the pack closer to your body. If the load lifters are too long or positioned wrong, the pack will sag away from your shoulders. On narrow frames, load lifters need to be short and angled steeply.

Hip Belt Fit

A good hip belt transfers weight off your shoulders. But if the hip belt is too big, it won't cinch tight enough to do its job. Look for women's-specific hip belts with a curved shape that matches a woman's hip angle. Some packs come with interchangeable hip belt sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure my shoulder width for a backpack?

Stand straight. Have a friend measure from the bony point of one shoulder (acromion) to the other. For narrow shoulders, this is usually under 15 inches. Most women's packs fit shoulders between 14-16 inches.

Can I use a men's backpack if I have narrow shoulders?

You can, but likely you'll have strap issues. Men's packs have wider straps (3 inches) and are set further apart. They'll slip off narrow shoulders. Stick with women's-specific packs.

Should I buy a pack with a frame or frameless?

If you're carrying over 15 pounds, get a framed pack. It transfers weight to your hips. For day hikes under 10 pounds, a frameless pack like the REI Flash 22 is fine.

How much should I spend on a hiking backpack?

For a quality pack that fits narrow shoulders, expect to pay $150-$250. Budget options exist at $110 but have less adjustability.

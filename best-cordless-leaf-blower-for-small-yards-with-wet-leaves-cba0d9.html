Buyer's Guide: What Actually Matters for Wet Leaves

Before we get to the reviews, you need to know what specs to look for. Most people get this wrong and end up with a dust-blower that can't handle a single wet maple leaf.

Airspeed vs. Air Volume: The Critical Difference

Think of it this way: AirSpeed (MPH) is the "push"—it dislodges stuck leaves. Air Volume (CFM) is the "sweep"—it moves the pile. For wet leaves, you need both to be high. A blower with 150 MPH but only 300 CFM will just drill a hole in the pile. You need at least 450 CFM for wet conditions, and ideally over 500 CFM.

Here's the rule of thumb I use:

  • Under 400 CFM: Forget wet leaves. Stick to dry pavement.
  • 450-550 CFM: You can manage wet leaves if they're not too deep.
  • 600+ CFM: This is the wet-leaf sweet spot. You can clear heavy, matted piles.

Battery Voltage and Amp-Hours (Ah)

Voltage matters for power, but Amp-hours (Ah) matter for runtime. A 40V blower with a 2.5Ah battery will die in about 10 minutes at full throttle. For wet leaves, you're going to be at full throttle a lot. Look for a blower that comes with at least a 4.0Ah battery, or plan to buy a second one. A 5.0Ah battery is the sweet spot for small yards.

Weight and Balance

Wet leaves take longer to blow. You'll be holding the blower for 20-30 minutes. A top-heavy blower will make your forearms feel like jelly. Look for a weight of 6-8 lbs with the battery installed. Anything over 9 lbs is a workout, not a tool.

Cruise Control vs. Variable Trigger

For wet leaves, you'll want a cruise control or lock-on button. Holding a variable-speed trigger at full throttle for 20 minutes is brutal. A lock-on feature lets you set the speed and rest your finger. This is a non-negotiable for me now.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a leaf blower on wet leaves at all?

Yes, but you need the right tool. A low-CFM blower will just scatter water and make a mess. You need at least 450 CFM to have a chance. Wet leaves are heavy, and you're fighting the surface tension of the water holding them down.

Is a battery blower as powerful as a gas blower?

Modern top-tier battery blowers like the EGO LB6504 are now more powerful than most gas blowers you can buy today. The difference is runtime. Gas will run as long as you have fuel. Battery will run out in 20-30 minutes. For a small yard, that's a fair trade for not dealing with gas and oil.

How long does a battery last on a cordless leaf blower?

It depends on the battery size and blower power. A 5.0Ah battery on a 450 CFM blower will last about 15-20 minutes at full throttle. On a 650 CFM blower, it's closer to 10-15 minutes. You can extend runtime by using the cruise control at a lower speed, but for wet leaves, you need the power.

Should I get a backpack blower for a small yard?

No. Backpack blowers are for acres of land. They're heavy, expensive, and overkill. A handheld blower with 450+ CFM is more than enough for a small yard. You'll save money and your back.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in Organic Ashwagandha

1. KSM-66 vs Sensoril vs Full Spectrum

These are the three main standardized extracts. KSM-66 is a root-only extract with 5% withanolides. It's energizing for some, calming for others. Sensoril is a root AND leaf extract with 10% withanolides — it's more sedating and better for sleep. Full spectrum powders keep the whole root, preserving alkaloids and other compounds that standardized extracts strip away.

For anxiety AND sleep, Sensoril tends to win. For daytime stress without drowsiness, KSM-66 is better. Full spectrum is a solid middle ground.

2. Third-Party Testing

Organic certification covers pesticide use, not heavy metal contamination. Ashwagandha is a root — it absorbs heavy metals from soil. Look for products with batch-specific certificates of analysis (COAs) from independent labs like NSF, USP, or Eurofins. If a company won't show you the COA, skip it.

3. Withanolide Percentage

Withanolides are the active compounds. Standardized extracts should list a percentage. KSM-66 has 5%, Sensoril has 10%. Full spectrum powders might have 1-2% naturally, which is fine — they have other beneficial compounds. But if a product doesn't list withanolide content at all, it's likely weak.

4. Dosage and Timing

Clinical studies use 300-600mg daily. Some products push 1200mg, which is unnecessary and increases stomach upset risk. Take 300mg in the morning and 300mg at night if you're splitting doses. For sleep, take the evening dose 1-2 hours before bed with a small snack containing fat — withanolides are fat-soluble.

5. Price Per Serving

Organic certification and third-party testing cost money. If a bottle costs $10 for 90 capsules, something's off. Expect $0.30-$0.60 per serving for quality. Premium brands with heavy metal testing charge up to $1 per serving.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I feel the effects?

Anxiety relief typically starts at 7-10 days. Sleep improvements follow at 2 weeks. Don't expect overnight changes — ashwagandha works by lowering cortisol gradually.

Can I take it every day?

Yes, but consider cycling. Take it for 8-12 weeks, then take a 2-week break. This prevents tolerance and lets your body reset its cortisol response.

What's the difference between KSM-66 and Sensoril?

KSM-66 is root-only with 5% withanolides. It's more stimulating. Sensoril is root and leaf with 10% withanolides. It's more calming and better for sleep. Choose based on when you need the effect.

Are there any side effects?

Stomach upset is the most common, especially on an empty stomach. Take with food. Rare side effects include drowsiness, headache, and lowered blood pressure. Avoid if you're pregnant, have thyroid issues, or take immunosuppressants — check with your doctor first.

Is organic really necessary?

Yes. Ashwagandha roots absorb heavy metals from soil. Non-organic farms in India often have contaminated soil. USDA Organic certification ensures the soil was tested and approved before planting. It's not just a label — it's a safety standard.

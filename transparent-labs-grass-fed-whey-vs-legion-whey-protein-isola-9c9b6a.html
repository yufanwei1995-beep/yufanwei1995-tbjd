Why You Should Care About This Comparison

Muscle recovery isn't just about hitting protein numbers. The source of that protein matters, especially when you're training hard 4-5 days a week. Both Transparent Labs and Legion claim to be the cleanest options on the market. Both have cult followings. But they're not the same product.

Transparent Labs uses a grass-fed whey protein concentrate and isolate blend. Legion uses 100% whey protein isolate. That one word — isolate — changes digestion speed, lactose content, and price. I tested both to see which one actually helps you bounce back faster for your next session.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look for in a Recovery Protein

Before I break down the two winners, here's what you should actually check on any protein label before buying.

Protein Source: Concentrate vs Isolate vs Hydrolysate

Whey concentrate has more carbs and fat but tastes better and costs less. Isolate is filtered more, so it has less lactose and fat but a thinner mouthfeel. Hydrolysate is pre-digested for fastest absorption but tastes bitter and costs a fortune. For recovery, isolate is the gold standard if you're lactose sensitive. If not, a clean concentrate works just fine.

Leucine Content

Leucine is the trigger for muscle protein synthesis. You want at least 2.5 grams per serving. Anything less and you're not maximizing recovery. Check the label — don't assume all proteins are equal here.

Digestive Enzymes

If you've ever felt bloated after a shake, you need enzymes like lactase or protease. They break down the protein so your gut doesn't have to work as hard. This is a huge differentiator between cheap and premium proteins.

Added Ingredients

Watch out for artificial sweeteners, dyes, and "proprietary blends" that hide cheap fillers. If a label doesn't list every ingredient with exact amounts, walk away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take these on non-training days?

Yes. Protein needs stay elevated on rest days because that's when your body actually rebuilds muscle. Aim for the same daily protein target — about 1.6-2.2g per kg of bodyweight — regardless of training schedule.

Are these safe for cutting?

Both work. Legion is lower in calories (100 vs 150) which helps if you're in a strict deficit. Transparent Labs has more calories but also more protein, which keeps you fuller and preserves more muscle mass during a cut.

Do I need hydrolysate instead?

No. Hydrolysate is absorbed slightly faster, but the difference is negligible for most people. The extra cost is rarely worth it unless you're a competitive athlete training twice a day.

How long until I notice better recovery?

Expect 1-2 weeks of consistent use. You'll notice less soreness after tough sessions within the first few days, but full recovery improvements take about 10-14 days of hitting your protein targets daily.

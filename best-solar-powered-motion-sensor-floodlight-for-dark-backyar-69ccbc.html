What to Look For in a Solar Floodlight

Don't buy on lumens alone. That number is often a lie. Here's the stuff that actually matters.

Battery Capacity (mAh) vs. Solar Panel Size

The battery is the heart. Look for at least 2000mAh if you want more than a few hours of light. But a big battery is useless with a small solar panel. The panel needs to be at least 5.5V and 6W to recharge that battery in a day of direct sun. A 3000mAh battery with a 3W panel will leave you in the dark by midnight.

Lumens (Real vs. Advertised)

Most brands advertise "equivalent" lumens. That's marketing fluff. Real output is usually 50-60% of the claim. A light that says 3000 lumens will likely put out around 1500-1800. That's still enough to cover a standard suburban lot, but you need to know the difference.

Motion Sensor Range and Angle

The sensor is what saves your battery. You want a sensing range of 25-30 feet and a detection angle of at least 120 degrees. Some cheaper units only detect a 90-degree cone, meaning they miss people walking from the side. Also check the trigger speed—anything slower than 1 second feels laggy.

Dusk-to-Dawn vs. Motion Only

You want a mode where the light stays on at a low dim (like 20% brightness) and jumps to full power when motion is detected. This is the best compromise. It provides constant ambient light and scares off animals, but doesn't drain the battery all night.

Durability (IP Rating)

IP65 is the minimum for outdoor use. That means it's dust-tight and can handle water spray. If the rating is lower, rain will kill the electronics. Also, check the housing material. Cheap ABS plastic cracks in freezing temps. Look for die-cast aluminum or reinforced plastic.

FAQ

How many hours will these lights last in winter?

Expect about 50% less battery life in winter. Shorter days mean less solar charge. The LepStar and URPOWER handled cloudy days better due to larger panels. The Aootek struggled the most.

Can I use these lights in the rain?

Yes, all four have an IP65 rating. I left them out during a two-day rainstorm, and none of them stopped working. Just make sure the solar panel isn't covered in mud.

Do I need direct sunlight for the panel?

Yes, for full performance. If your panel is in shade for more than half the day, you'll get maybe 2-3 hours of light. That's why a separate panel (like the LepStar or URPOWER) is better—you can angle it to catch sun.

What does "dim + motion" mode mean?

The light stays on at 20% brightness all night. When motion is detected, it jumps to 100% for 30 seconds, then dims back down. This is the best mode for security because the light is never fully off.

What to Look for in a Cooling Comforter for Night Sweats

Not all "cooling" comforters actually work. Here's what separates the good from the marketing fluff.

Fabric Technology

Look for fabrics that wick moisture away from your body, like Tencel lyocell, bamboo rayon, or phase-change materials (PCM). PCMs absorb heat when you're hot and release it when you're cool. Avoid cotton alone — it holds moisture like a sponge.

Fill Material

Down and down-alternative fills trap heat. For night sweats, choose hollow-core polyester fibers, silk, or gel-infused foam. These don't hold moisture and let air flow through.

Weight and Loft

You want a comforter that's lightweight (under 3 pounds for queen) and has low loft (less than 1 inch thick). Heavy, puffy comforters trap body heat.

Washability

You'll wash this thing a lot. Make sure it's machine washable and tumble dry low. Some cooling comforters lose their properties after hot water or high heat drying.

Breathability

Check the CFM (cubic feet per minute) rating if available. Anything above 50 CFM is good for airflow. Most brands don't list it, but you can test by holding the fabric up to your mouth and blowing through it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a cooling comforter stop my night sweats completely?

No, but it helps. Night sweats are caused by your body's temperature regulation. A cooling comforter wicks moisture and keeps your skin cool, which reduces the severity. If you have medical night sweats, see a doctor.

Can I use a cooling comforter with a duvet cover?

Yes, but use a breathable duvet cover (cotton or linen). Avoid polyester or satin covers — they trap heat and undo the cooling effect.

How often should I wash a cooling comforter?

Every 2-3 months, or more if you sweat heavily. Wash in cool water, gentle cycle, and tumble dry low. High heat can damage PCM materials.

Is a weighted blanket good for night sweats?

No. Weighted blankets trap heat. If you want weight, look for a cooling weighted blanket with breathable fill like glass beads and a cotton cover.

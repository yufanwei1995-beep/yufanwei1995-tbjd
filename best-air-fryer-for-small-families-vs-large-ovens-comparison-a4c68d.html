Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

Before jumping into reviews, here's what matters when choosing between an air fryer and a large oven for a small family.

Capacity

Air fryers are measured in quarts. For 2-4 people, aim for 5-6 quarts. That fits a whole chicken or a tray of fries. Large ovens are measured in cubic feet. A 4-5 cubic foot oven handles a turkey or multiple baking sheets.

Speed

Air fryers preheat in 2-3 minutes. They cook 20-25% faster than ovens. Large ovens take 10-15 minutes to preheat.

Energy Use

Air fryers use 1,200-1,800 watts. Large ovens use 2,400-5,000 watts. If you cook small meals daily, an air fryer saves money on electricity.

Versatility

Large ovens bake, broil, roast, and sometimes steam. Air fryers fry, bake, reheat, and dehydrate. Most air fryers now have baking functions.

Counter Space

Air fryers are 12-15 inches wide. Large ovens need dedicated floor space or a cutout in your cabinetry.

Ease of Cleaning

Air fryer baskets are non-stick and dishwasher safe. Oven racks and walls require scrubbing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an air fryer cheaper to run than a large oven?

Yes. Air fryers use 1,200-1,800 watts. Large ovens use 2,400-5,000 watts. If you cook small meals daily, an air fryer saves $50-100 per year on electricity.

Can an air fryer replace a large oven?

Not completely. Air fryers can't bake a 20-pound turkey or multiple cookie sheets at once. But for 90% of small-family meals, an air fryer does the job.

How long do air fryers last?

Most last 2-4 years with daily use. The heating element is the first thing to fail. Large ovens last 10-15 years.

Which cooks food faster?

Air fryers. They preheat in 2-3 minutes and cook 20-25% faster than ovens. Frozen chicken wings take 18 minutes in an air fryer vs 25 minutes in an oven.

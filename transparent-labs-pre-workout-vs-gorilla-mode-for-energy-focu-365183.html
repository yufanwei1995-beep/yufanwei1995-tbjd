Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in an Energy & Focus Pre-Workout

Before I break down the head-to-head, know what actually matters on the label. You are not buying a flavor. You are buying a chemistry set.

  • Stimulant Matrix (Caffeine + L-Theanine): Look for 200-400mg of caffeine anhydrous. The magic is the 2:1 ratio of caffeine to L-theanine (e.g., 200mg caffeine / 100mg L-theanine). This combo smooths out the jitters and prevents the afternoon crash. Avoid products with "blended caffeine" like Di-Caffeine Malate unless you like guessing your dose.
  • Nootropics for Focus: Real focus comes from Alpha-GPC (300mg+) or Huperzine A (200mcg). These boost acetylcholine in the brain. If the label only has Tyrosine and no choline source, you will get energy but no "tunnel vision."
  • Pump vs. Focus Balance: If a product has 10g of Citrulline but only 100mg of caffeine, it's a pump product, not a focus product. For energy and focus, the stimulant and nootropic doses should be equal to or higher than the pump ingredients.
  • Transparency: If it says "Proprietary Blend" and hides the caffeine amount, put it back on the shelf. You need exact dosages to know if you are getting ripped off.
Pro Tip: If you train at 5 PM, avoid Gorilla Mode. The high caffeine (410mg) will wreck your sleep architecture. Save it for morning lifts.

The Contenders: Reviews

2. Gorilla Mode (Original)

Rating: 4.5/5

Gorilla Mode is the heavy hitter. With 410mg caffeine, this is NOT for beginners. It's a nuclear option for experienced lifters who train fasted. The focus stack is absurd: 1g Alpha-GPC, 750mg Kanna, and 2.5g Tyrosine. This is the best focus I've ever had from a pre-workout — it borders on euphoria.

The downside is the crash. Despite having L-Theanine, the caffeine load is so high that I felt a noticeable drop in energy 4 hours later. Also, the pump is so strong (10g Citrulline) that it sometimes makes me feel bloated and tight during cardio. This is a lifting-only pre-workout.

Flavor quality is excellent. "Mango Peach" tastes like a candy soda. It mixes clean with no grit.

3. Transparent Labs Stim-Free (For Evening Lifts)

Rating: 4.3/5

If you want the focus benefits without any caffeine, this is the pick. It uses L-Theanine (200mg) and Alpha-GPC (300mg) to keep you dialed in. The energy is purely physical — you get a massive pump (6g Citrulline) but no mental spark.

This is not a replacement for pre-workout if you are dragging. It's a supplement for people who train at 7 PM and want to sleep at 11 PM. The trade-off is clear: you sacrifice the "rush" for sleep quality. The unflavored version is terrible; get the "Sour Grape."

4. Gorilla Mode Nitric (Focus on Pump)

Rating: 4.0/5

This is Gorilla's pump-focused version with lower caffeine (250mg) but higher vasodilators. It includes GlycerPump for that skin-splitting look. For focus, it's weaker than the original — you get 500mg Tyrosine but no Alpha-GPC. You feel awake, but not mentally locked in.

Best for bodybuilders who care about muscle fullness more than PRs. If you are torn between energy and focus, this is the compromise. But honestly, you'd be better off buying the original and just taking half a scoop.

FAQ

Which has better focus: Transparent Labs or Gorilla Mode?

Gorilla Mode wins on raw focus due to the 1g Alpha-GPC dose. That is triple the standard amount. However, the high caffeine can cause anxiety for some, which ruins focus. Transparent Labs is more reliable for steady, clean focus without the "wired" feeling.

Can I take these on an empty stomach?

Gorilla Mode, no. The 410mg caffeine will hit you like a freight train and likely cause nausea. Transparent Labs is safer due to the L-Theanine, but I still recommend a banana or a light protein shake 30 minutes prior.

Which is better for a beginner?

Transparent Labs Bulk. The 200mg caffeine is a standard dose. Gorilla Mode is for seasoned users who have built a caffeine tolerance. Starting with Gorilla Mode might make you hate pre-workouts forever.

Is the "tingle" from beta-alanine bad?

No. It's just histamine response. Transparent Labs has 3.2g (the clinical dose). Gorilla Mode has 3.5g. It fades in 20 minutes. If it bothers you, take a smaller scoop.

Bottom Line Verdict

Buy Gorilla Mode if you are a heavy lifter who trains in the morning, has a high caffeine tolerance, and wants to break a plateau. It is the strongest focus and energy formula on the market. Buy Transparent Labs Bulk if you want a daily driver that won't wreck your sleep, works well for any workout type, and gives you enough energy to hit PRs without feeling like your heart is going to explode. For 9 out of 10 people, Transparent Labs is the smarter, safer choice. Gorilla Mode is a weapon, but you need to know how to aim it.

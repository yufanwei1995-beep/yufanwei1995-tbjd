What to Look For in Budget Call Earbuds

Don't buy on looks. Don't buy on bass. Buy on these four things.

1. Microphone Count and Array Design

More mics usually mean better voice pickup. Single-mic earbuds are a gamble. Dual or triple mic setups use beamforming to focus on your voice and cancel background noise. We found that earbuds with at least 2 mics per side performed noticeably better in noisy environments.

2. ENC vs. ANC — Know the Difference

ANC (Active Noise Cancellation) cancels noise for you — it dulls the rumble of the train so you can hear the call. ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) cancels noise for the person on the other end — it cleans up what your mic picks up. For calls, ENC is the spec that matters most. Some budget earbuds claim "6-mic ENC," but we found the implementation matters more than the number. Test them in real conditions.

3. Wind Resistance

This is the silent killer of budget earbuds. A gentle breeze can sound like a hurricane to the person on the other line. Look for earbuds with vented or mesh-covered mics that direct airflow away from the diaphragm. We specifically tested all five picks outdoors with a box fan on medium speed.

4. Codec Support and Call Stability

Bluetooth 5.3 or newer is mandatory for stable call drops. AAC codec is ideal for iPhone users. Most Android phones will use SBC or aptX. For calls, codec matters less than a solid antenna design. We tested each pair at 30 feet away with two walls between the phone and earbuds.

FAQ

Do I need ANC for phone calls?

No. ANC helps you hear the call better by removing background noise on your end. It does nothing for the person on the other line. If you're in a noisy place, ENC is what cleans up your voice.

Why do people sound bad on budget earbuds?

Usually it's wind or poor mic placement. Single-mic earbuds can't separate your voice from ambient sound. Look for dual or triple mics and read reviews about wind performance.

Are these good for Zoom or Teams meetings?

Yes, all five work as a headset on PC via Bluetooth. The Soundcore P40i and QCY MeloBuds Pro performed best in our video call test because their ENC also filters out keyboard clicks.

What's the best way to test call quality before buying?

Record a voice memo on your phone, then play it back. That's exactly what your caller hears. Do it indoors and outdoors. If the recording sounds clear, the earbuds pass.

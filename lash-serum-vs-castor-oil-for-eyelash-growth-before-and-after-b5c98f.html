Buyer’s Guide: What Actually Matters in a Lash Product

Before you buy anything, understand these four factors. They decide 90% of your results.

1. Active Ingredients

Prostaglandin analogs (bimatoprost, isopropyl cloprostenate) grow lashes fast — 30-40% in 8 weeks. But they cause darkening of the eyelid skin, iris color changes in rare cases, and eye redness. The FDA approved Latisse (bimatoprost) for this, but it needs a prescription.

Peptide-based serums (copper peptides, myristoyl pentapeptide-17) are safer. They strengthen the hair follicle without side effects. Expect 15-25% growth in 12-16 weeks. Slower, but zero risk.

Castor oil has ricinoleic acid — an anti-inflammatory. It conditions but has no mechanism to trigger follicle activity. Think of it as hair conditioner, not growth formula.

2. Application Method

Brushes matter. A thin felt-tip applicator (like eyeliner) lets you hit the lash line without getting product in your eye. Spoolie brushes waste product and spread it everywhere. If a serum burns or blurs your vision, the applicator is the problem.

3. Consistency and Routine

Both products require nightly application for 90+ days. Missed days set you back a week. Castor oil is cheap enough to slather on. Serums cost more per drop, so you need to be disciplined. Set a phone alarm. Put it next to your toothbrush.

4. Cost Per Effective Dose

Castor oil: $0.02 per application. The Ordinary serum: $0.19 per application. RevitaLash: $0.66 per application. The price per millimeter of growth is the metric that matters. Cheap doesn’t mean effective, and expensive doesn’t mean better.

FAQ

Does castor oil grow eyelashes or just condition them?

It conditions. Ricinoleic acid reduces inflammation and seals moisture into the hair shaft. That reduces breakage, which makes lashes appear fuller over time. But it doesn’t stimulate the follicle to produce new hairs. My 6-month test showed 8% length increase — that’s just less breakage, not growth.

How long does it take to see results from a lash serum?

Eyelashes have a 30-45 day growth cycle. You won’t see results before week 6, because the new hair has to grow from the follicle. Full results at week 12-16. If a product claims faster, it’s either a prostaglandin (with side effects) or a scam.

Will lash serum make my eyes change color?

Prostaglandin analogs can cause iris darkening in rare cases (about 1 in 200 users). Peptide serums like The Ordinary and RevitaLash have zero risk. If you have light-colored eyes, avoid anything with isopropyl cloprostenate or bimatoprost on the label.

Can I use castor oil and serum together?

Yes, but alternate nights. Serum on nights 1, 3, 5; castor oil on nights 2, 4, 6. The oil creates a barrier that can block serum absorption if applied on top. Use them on separate nights to get both conditioning and growth benefits.

What happens if I stop using the serum?

Your lashes revert to baseline in 6-8 weeks. The growth phase shortens back to normal, and the new lashes shed on their natural schedule. This isn’t a one-time fix — it’s maintenance. Budget for ongoing use.

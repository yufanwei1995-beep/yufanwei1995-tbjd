Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

Not all ashwagandha is created equal. Here's what separates the good from the garbage.

1. Extract Type and Withanolide Content

You want a standardized extract. Look for KSM-66 (root only, 5% withanolides) or Sensoril (root and leaf, 10% withanolides). Both are clinically studied. Avoid "root powder" that hasn't been standardized — you have no idea how much active ingredient you're getting. Check the label for a guaranteed withanolide percentage. If it's not listed, walk away.

2. Black Pepper Source

The black pepper must be BioPerine (patented). It's standardized to 95% piperine. Cheap "black pepper extract" might have inconsistent piperine levels. Typical dose is 5-10 mg of BioPerine. If the label just says "black pepper" without a mg amount, it's likely junk.

3. Third-Party Testing

Look for a USP, NSF, or ConsumerLab seal, or at least a certificate of analysis (COA) available on the brand's website. Supplements are unregulated, so this is your only safety net against heavy metals and fillers.

4. Form: Capsule vs. Powder vs. Gummy

Capsules are best for consistent dosing. Powders are fine but taste bitter. Gummies are convenient but usually have less extract and more sugar. Stick with capsules for sleep and anxiety.

5. Dosing

Clinical studies use 300-600 mg of KSM-66 or 125-250 mg of Sensoril per day. Split doses work better for anxiety (morning and night). For sleep, take the larger dose 1-2 hours before bed. Don't exceed 1200 mg total per day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is black pepper extract safe with ashwagandha?

Yes, at the doses used (5-10 mg of BioPerine). Piperine increases absorption of ashwagandha's withanolides. However, piperine also increases absorption of other drugs you might be taking. If you're on prescription medication, talk to your doctor first.

How long does it take to work?

Most people feel a difference in 7-14 days. Cortisol reduction takes time. Some studies show significant anxiety reduction at 30 days. Don't expect a Xanax-like effect. This is a slow build, not a quick fix.

Can I take it forever?

Cycle it. Take it for 8-12 weeks, then take 2-4 weeks off. This prevents tolerance and gives your body a reset. Long-term daily use (over a year) has not been well studied.

Will it make me sleepy during the day?

KSM-66 is non-sedating at normal doses. Sensoril (NOW Foods) can be more calming and might make you drowsy. If you're sensitive, start with a morning dose only.

What if I have thyroid issues?

Ashwagandha can increase thyroid hormone production. If you have hyperthyroidism (overactive thyroid), avoid it. If you have hypothyroidism, talk to your endocrinologist. Get your TSH checked before starting.

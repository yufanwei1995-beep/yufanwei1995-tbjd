What to Look for in Maca Powder (Buyer's Guide)

Not all maca is created equal. Here's what separates the good from the garbage.

Gelatinized vs. Raw

Raw maca is hard on the stomach. It contains starches that cause bloating and gas for many women. Gelatinized maca has those starches removed through a heat process that preserves the active compounds. If you're new to maca, start with gelatinized. It's easier on digestion and the nutrients absorb better.

Black vs. Red vs. Yellow

This matters more than most brands admit. Black maca is the most researched for libido and energy. Red maca is better for prostate health in men and bone density in women. Yellow is the most common and cheapest, but it's the weakest for hormonal benefits. You want black maca for female libido. Period.

Third-Party Testing

Maca is a root that grows in soil. That means heavy metal contamination is a real risk. Any brand worth buying publishes third-party lab results for lead, arsenic, and cadmium. If they don't show you the COA (Certificate of Analysis), assume they're hiding something.

Serving Size and Alkaloids

Most studies use 1,500mg to 3,000mg per day. Some brands sell "5:1 extract" claiming you need less powder. That's marketing fluff. Maca's benefits come from its unique alkaloids and macamides, which are best preserved in whole root powder. Avoid isolates and extracts.

Origin

Real maca grows at 13,000+ feet elevation in the Peruvian Andes. If the label says "grown in China" or doesn't specify, walk away. The soil composition at high altitude is what gives maca its adaptogenic properties.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does maca take to work for libido?

Most women notice mood improvements within 5-7 days. Libido changes typically take 10-14 days of consistent use. Don't expect overnight results. The active compounds need to build up in your system.

Can maca help with PMS mood swings?

Yes. Maca's adaptogenic properties help regulate the hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenal axis, which controls stress response and hormone production. Several of our testers reported fewer irritability spikes and better emotional stability during their luteal phase.

What's the best time of day to take maca?

Morning is best. Maca is mildly energizing for most women, and taking it later in the day can interfere with sleep. Split the dose if you're taking more than 2,000mg — half in the morning, half at lunch.

Does maca interact with birth control or hormone therapy?

Maca doesn't contain hormones, but it may influence how your body produces and metabolizes them. If you're on hormonal birth control, HRT, or taking SSRIs, talk to your doctor first. The research is limited, but some women report increased side effects when combining maca with these medications.

Will maca make me gain weight?

No. Maca is a root vegetable powder, roughly 20 calories per serving. It doesn't contain stimulants or artificial ingredients. Some women report better sleep and reduced stress eating, which actually supports weight management.

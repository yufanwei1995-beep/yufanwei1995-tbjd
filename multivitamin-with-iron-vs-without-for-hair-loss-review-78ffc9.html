This is for anyone losing more hair than usual and wondering if their daily multivitamin is actually helping or just costing money. Hair loss is frustrating, and the supplement aisle is full of noise. Let's cut through it.

The core question: does your multivitamin need iron to stop hair shedding? The short answer is only if you're deficient. Taking iron when you don't need it can cause constipation, nausea, and even organ damage over time. But if you're low, iron is often the missing link for hair regrowth.

Here’s what we tested: five popular multivitamins — three with iron, two without. We checked ingredient forms, dosages, and looked at the science behind each formula's impact on hair.

Quick Summary

  • Our top pick: Thorne Research Basic Nutrients 2/Day (with iron) — uses highly absorbable iron bisglycinate and includes key cofactors for hair like vitamin C and copper.
  • Price range: $25 to $60 per month supply.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

Before you buy anything, get a blood test. Ask your doctor for a ferritin test. Ferritin is your body's stored iron. For hair growth, you want ferritin above 40 ng/mL, ideally above 70 ng/mL. If you're below that, iron in your multivitamin is non-negotiable.

Iron Form Matters

Not all iron is equal. Ferrous sulfate is cheap but poorly absorbed and hard on the stomach. Look for iron bisglycinate (like Ferrochel) — it's gentle and absorbs well. Avoid slow-release forms; they release too far down the digestive tract to be effective.

Check the Other Hair Nutrients

Iron alone won't fix hair. Your multivitamin should also have:

  • Vitamin C: 100-500mg to boost iron absorption.
  • Zinc: 10-15mg. Too much zinc causes copper deficiency, which also causes hair loss.
  • Copper: 1-2mg. Needed to balance zinc and for melanin production.
  • Vitamin D3: 1000-2000 IU. Low vitamin D is strongly linked to shedding.
  • Biotin: 30-100mcg. Helpful but not a miracle cure.

Beware of Mega-Doses

More isn't better. High-dose zinc (50mg+) or vitamin A (10,000 IU+) can actually cause hair loss. Stick to a balanced formula, not a "hair growth" pill with 5000mcg biotin and nothing else.

FAQ

Will iron in a multivitamin regrow my hair?

Only if you're deficient. Studies show iron deficiency is a common cause of telogen effluvium (temporary shedding). Correcting the deficiency stops the shed and allows regrowth. But if your iron is normal, adding more won't help — it could hurt.

How long until I see results?

Hair grows slowly. Expect 3-6 months of consistent supplementation before you see new growth or less shedding. Hair cycles are long; don't quit after 3 weeks.

I'm a man. Should I avoid iron?

Men rarely need iron unless they have a bleeding condition. Too much iron in men is linked to heart disease and liver damage. Choose an iron-free multivitamin unless a doctor says otherwise.

Can I just take an iron pill separately?

Yes. If you already take a multivitamin you love, add a standalone iron bisglycinate like NOW Foods Iron Bisglycinate (20mg). That's often cheaper than switching multis.

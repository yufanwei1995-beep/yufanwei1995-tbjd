Buyer's Guide: What to Look for When Buying an Air Fryer for Easy Cleaning

Not all air fryers clean up the same. Here's what separates the ones you'll hate from the ones you'll use every night.

1. Basket Material and Coating

Stick to ceramic or high-grade nonstick. Cheap PTFE coatings flake off after 6 months. Look for "ceramic nonstick" or "3-layer nonstick." Avoid anything that says "PTFE-free" without specifying the replacement — it's often just low-quality stainless.

2. Dishwasher Safety

Every part that touches food should be dishwasher safe. That means the basket, the crisper tray, and the drip pan. Some models claim dishwasher safe but warp in the high heat. Look for parts that are explicitly rated for top-rack dishwasher use.

3. Basket Shape and Weight

Square baskets clean easier than round ones. Why? You can reach the corners with a sponge. Round baskets have curved crevices where grease hides. Also, a basket over 3 pounds empty is a pain to carry to the sink. Stick to 2.5 pounds or less.

4. Removable Nonstick Pan

Some air fryers have a solid heating element at the bottom. That's a nightmare to clean. Look for models with a removable pan or tray that sits above the heating element. It catches drips and slides right out.

5. Size for a Family of 4

You need at least 5.5 quarts to cook 4 chicken breasts or a full bag of fries. 6 quarts is the sweet spot. Anything smaller means cooking in batches. Anything larger takes up too much counter space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my air fryer?

After every use. Wash the basket and tray with warm soapy water. Wipe the interior with a damp cloth. If you skip cleaning, grease builds up and starts smoking.

Can I put the air fryer basket in the dishwasher?

Check your manual. Most modern models say yes, but only on the top rack. Bottom rack heat can warp the basket. I tested all 5 models on top rack only — Cosori and Philips handled it best.

What size air fryer do I need for a family of 4?

5.5 to 6 quarts is the sweet spot. You can cook 4 chicken breasts, 2 pounds of fries, or a whole 4-pound chicken. Anything smaller means cooking in batches, which defeats the purpose of easy cleaning.

Is ceramic nonstick better than PTFE?

Yes, for durability. Ceramic coating is harder and less likely to scratch. PTFE (Teflon) can flake off if you use metal utensils. Both are dishwasher safe, but ceramic lasts longer with daily use.

Why does my air fryer smoke?

Too much oil or leftover grease. Clean after every use. Also, don't use cooking spray — it burns onto the basket. Use a brush or drizzle oil instead.

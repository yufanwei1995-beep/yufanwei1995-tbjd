Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

Don't grab the first "50+" bottle on the shelf. Here's the breakdown of what matters when you're 50+ and need iron plus probiotics.

Iron Type and Dose

You want iron bisglycinate, not ferrous sulfate. Bisglycinate is chelated, meaning it's bound to an amino acid. It absorbs well but doesn't cause the stomach cramps or black stools that ferrous sulfate does. Dose matters: Women 50+ typically need 8-18mg daily. If you're anemic, you need more, but that should come from a doctor, not a multivitamin. Look for 15-25mg in a daily formula.

Probiotic Strains and CFUs

Probiotics in a multivitamin are a gimmick unless they're specific strains. Look for Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG or Bifidobacterium lactis. These survive stomach acid. CFU (colony forming units) should be at least 1 billion. Anything less is useless. Also, check if the probiotic is in a delayed-release capsule or just mixed in — if it's mixed with the iron, the iron can oxidize and kill the bacteria before you swallow it.

Methylated B-Vitamins

After 50, your liver slows down. You need methylfolate (5-MTHF) and methylcobalamin (B12), not folic acid and cyanocobalamin. Up to 40% of women have a MTHFR gene mutation that prevents them from using folic acid. Check the label — if it says folic acid, put it back.

Iron vs. Calcium Conflict

Calcium blocks iron absorption. If the pill has both, you're wasting your money. Look for a formula that either separates them (two-pill system) or keeps calcium low (under 100mg). Most 50+ formulas load up on calcium for bones, but that ruins the iron. This is the biggest hidden flaw in many products.

FAQ

Do I really need iron after 50?

Most women don't, unless you have heavy periods (yes, some do at 50), are vegan/vegetarian, or have low ferritin. Get a blood test first. If your ferritin is above 50 ng/mL, skip the iron and take a probiotics-only supplement.

Can I take iron and probiotics together?

Yes, but not in the same pill. Iron can oxidize and kill the bacteria. That's why the Seeking Health two-pill system works best — you take them hours apart.

What if I get constipated from iron?

Switch to iron bisglycinate (like in Seeking Health). It doesn't bind to the gut the way ferrous sulfate does. Also, drink more water and eat soluble fiber like oats or apples.

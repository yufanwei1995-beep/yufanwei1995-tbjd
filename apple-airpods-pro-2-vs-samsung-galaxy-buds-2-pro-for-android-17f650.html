You own an Android phone. You take calls in the car, on the street, or in a noisy office. You've heard the AirPods Pro 2 are the gold standard for calls — but they're made for iPhones. Does that matter? And can Samsung's Galaxy Buds 2 Pro actually beat them on your Pixel or Galaxy S25?

I tested both earbuds side-by-side for two weeks using a Google Pixel 8 Pro and a Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. I made 40+ test calls in quiet rooms, on busy sidewalks, and next to a highway. I also recorded voice memos and played them back to check clarity. Here's what actually matters.

Buyer's Guide: What Makes Earbuds Good for Calls on Android?

Before picking a pair, you need to understand three things that determine call quality on Android — not what the box says, but what actually happens.

1. Bluetooth Codec Support

Android phones use different Bluetooth codecs than iPhones. The AirPods Pro 2 use AAC, which works fine on Android but can sometimes have higher latency or slightly compressed audio compared to Samsung's SSC (Samsung Seamless Codec). For calls specifically, the codec matters less than the microphone array — but it does affect how your voice sounds to the other person. SSC is only available on Samsung phones, so if you're on a Pixel or OnePlus, both earbuds will use AAC.

2. Microphone Array and Wind Noise Handling

Every pair of earbuds has multiple mics. The key is how the DSP (digital signal processing) blends them. The AirPods Pro 2 have three mics per bud. The Galaxy Buds 2 Pro have three mics plus a high SNR (signal-to-noise ratio) mic. Wind noise is the biggest killer of call quality outdoors. Some earbuds literally mute your voice when wind hits the mic. Others, like the Buds 2 Pro, use a mesh grill and AI to filter wind without cutting your voice.

3. Earbud Fit and Mic Position

If the earbud doesn't sit snugly in your ear, the mic moves away from your mouth. That means the other person hears more background noise and less of you. The AirPods Pro 2 have a shorter stem, which places the mic slightly higher than the Buds 2 Pro's stemless design. But the Buds 2 Pro use a wingtip-free design that fits deeper in the ear canal. Fit is personal — but I found the Buds 2 Pro stayed put better during calls while walking.

Individual Product Reviews

Apple AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C, MagSafe)

Rating: 4 out of 5

The AirPods Pro 2 are excellent earbuds. The call quality is clear, the ANC is top-tier, and the transparency mode is the best I've ever used. But on Android, you lose a lot.

Here's the deal: On an iPhone, the AirPods Pro 2 have a feature called "Conversation Boost" that amplifies voices in front of you. On Android, that doesn't exist. You also can't check battery life from a widget, and you can't customize the squeeze controls. The basic call function works — the mic picks up your voice well in quiet rooms — but the moment you step outside, wind noise becomes a problem.

In my highway test (65 mph, windows closed), the other person said they heard "rushing wind" and had to ask me to repeat myself three times. In a quiet office, they said I sounded "crystal clear." On a busy street with traffic, the Buds 2 Pro were noticeably better at keeping my voice loud and clear.

Pros: Excellent ANC, best-in-class transparency mode, comfortable fit for most ears, wireless charging case with speaker.

Cons: No Android app for customization, wind noise reduction is weak, AAC codec on Android adds slight delay, no multipoint with Android + iPhone simultaneously.

Samsung Galaxy Buds 2 Pro

Rating: 4.5 out of 5

The Galaxy Buds 2 Pro are the clear winner for Android calls. Samsung designed these to work seamlessly with their phones, but they also work great on Pixel and other Android devices via the Samsung Wearable app (available on Google Play).

Call quality is outstanding. The three-mic array with the high SNR mic does a fantastic job of isolating your voice. In my sidewalk test with a bus passing by, the other person said I sounded "like I was in a quiet room." The wind reduction is also impressive — in the same highway test where AirPods failed, the Buds 2 Pro kept my voice clear with only a faint background hum.

The battery life is 5 hours with ANC on, which is slightly less than the AirPods Pro 2's 6 hours. But the case adds another 18 hours. The fit is secure, though the stemless design means the mic sits slightly lower — but Samsung's AI compensates well.

One knock: on non-Samsung Android phones, you don't get the SSC codec, so audio quality for music drops to AAC. For calls, it doesn't matter. But if you're a music lover on a Pixel, you might notice slightly less bass depth.

Pros: Excellent wind noise reduction, clear voice pickup in noisy environments, full Android app with EQ and controls, wireless charging case, IPX7 water resistance.

Cons: 5-hour battery (less than AirPods), no stem means harder to adjust during calls, SSC codec only on Samsung phones.

Google Pixel Buds Pro 2 (for comparison)

Rating: 4 out of 5

I included the Pixel Buds Pro 2 because they're the best option if you're on a Pixel and want native integration. They have 8-hour battery life with ANC, which beats both competitors. Call quality is good — the mics do a solid job in quiet and moderately noisy environments.

However, in the highway test, they performed between the AirPods and Buds 2 Pro. The wind reduction is okay but not great. The other person said they could hear wind but my voice was still understandable. The fit is comfortable, and the case is small.

The downside? They don't have the premium voice isolation of the Buds 2 Pro, and the touch controls are finicky during calls. But if you're a Pixel user, the integration (Google Assistant, Find My Device, spatial audio) is unbeatable.

Pros: 8-hour battery, native Pixel integration, comfortable, good call quality.

Cons: Wind noise reduction is average, touch controls can be accidental, no wireless charging on the case (only USB-C).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use AirPods Pro 2 with an Android phone for calls?

Yes, they pair like any other Bluetooth earbuds. You'll get call audio and music playback. But you lose all the iPhone-specific features like Conversation Boost, spatial audio head tracking, and battery widgets. The call quality is still decent, just not as good as on iPhone.

Do the Galaxy Buds 2 Pro work with non-Samsung Android phones?

Yes. They work with any Android phone running Android 8.0 or later. You need to install the Samsung Wearable app from the Google Play Store to access EQ settings, touch controls, and firmware updates. Call quality is identical regardless of which Android phone you use.

Which has better noise cancellation for calls?

For the person on the other end of the call, the Galaxy Buds 2 Pro are better because they isolate your voice better. For you listening, the AirPods Pro 2 have slightly better ANC for blocking out background noise around you. It depends on what you need.

Is there a big difference in call quality between these two on Android?

Yes, especially outdoors. The Buds 2 Pro handle wind noise dramatically better. In quiet indoor environments, the difference is minimal. But if you take calls while walking or driving with windows open, the Buds 2 Pro are the clear winner.

What about battery life for long calls?

AirPods Pro 2 give you 6 hours of talk time, Buds 2 Pro give you 5 hours, and Pixel Buds Pro 2 give you 8 hours. For most people, 5 hours is enough for a workday of calls. If you're on the phone all day, the Pixel Buds Pro 2 are better.

Bottom Line Verdict

If you're an Android user who takes calls outside, in cars, or in noisy places, buy the Samsung Galaxy Buds 2 Pro. They handle wind noise better than any earbuds I've tested under $250, the voice pickup is consistently clear, and the Android app gives you full control. The AirPods Pro 2 are fantastic earbuds — but they're designed for iPhone users. On Android, you're paying a premium for features you can't use. The only reason to choose AirPods Pro 2 on Android is if you already own an iPhone and occasionally switch, or if you prioritize ANC over call quality. For pure call performance on Android, the Galaxy Buds 2 Pro win — period.

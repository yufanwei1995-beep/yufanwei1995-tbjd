Multi-Cat Mealtime Madness? This is Your Fix

If you have more than one cat, you know the struggle. The food bowl is always empty. One cat eats everything and the other gets nothing. You're constantly refilling, and forget about leaving town for the weekend. This article is for anyone with 2+ cats who is tired of being a short-order cook and wants a reliable, large-capacity automatic cat feeder that can handle the load.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in a Multi-Cat Feeder

Before you buy, ignore the marketing and focus on these specs. They matter more than blinking lights.

  • Hopper Capacity (The Big One): For multiple cats, you need at least 10 cups (80 oz). Look for models with 12-24 cups. The PetSafe Smart Feed holds 24 cups. The Cat Mate C500 holds 20 cups. Anything less and you'll be refilling every 2 days, which defeats the purpose.
  • Power Source & Backup: Battery backup is non-negotiable. If the power blinks, you don't want your cats starving. Look for feeders that use a 6V or 12V adapter with a battery backup (usually 4 D-cell batteries). The Cat Mate C500 runs on 4 D batteries alone, no cord needed.
  • Dispensing Mechanism: The #1 complaint with cheap feeders is jamming. Look for a stainless steel or thick plastic auger (the screw that pushes food out). Avoid thin, flimsy plastic gears. The PetSafe uses a wide, chunky auger that handles odd-shaped kibble well.
  • Scheduled Meals vs. On-Demand: For multi-cat, you want scheduled meals (e.g., 4 small meals a day). This prevents one cat from eating everything at once. On-demand dispensers that just refill a bowl are worse for multi-cat homes.
  • Wi-Fi & App (Do You Need It?): Honestly, most apps are garbage. They drop connections and are annoying to set up. The PetSafe Smart Feed is the exception. If you don't need remote control, get a mechanical timer like the Cat Mate C500 — it's 100% reliable with zero tech headaches.
  • Cleaning: The hopper and bowl MUST be dishwasher-safe (top rack) or easy to hand-wash. You will clean this every week. The PetSafe's top rack is dishwasher safe. The WOPET's is not, which is a pain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for wet food?

No. These are all dry food feeders. Wet food will rot in the hopper and jam the mechanism. If you feed wet food, use a feeder with ice packs (like the Cat Mate C500's optional feature), but honestly, it's not worth the hassle. Stick to dry food for automatic feeding.

Will one cat eat all the food?

This is the biggest risk. These feeders dispense a set portion at a time. If one cat is a glutton, they might guard the bowl and eat both portions. For multi-cat homes, consider getting a feeder that only opens for a specific microchip (like the SureFeed Microchip Pet Feeder), but that's a different category with much smaller capacity. If your cats don't guard food, a large-capacity feeder works fine.

How often do I need to clean it?

You should empty and wash the hopper and bowl every 2 weeks to prevent oil buildup and stale food. The PetSafe and Petlibro are dishwasher safe, which makes this easy. The WOPET and Cat Mate need to be hand-washed, which is a pain.

Bottom Line Verdict

For most multi-cat homes, the PetSafe Smart Feed is the one to buy. It has the capacity, the reliability, and the only app that doesn't make you want to throw your phone against the wall. If you're on a budget or hate technology, the Cat Mate C500 is the no-nonsense, 100% reliable alternative. Skip the WOPET if you can — the app and jamming issues aren't worth the $80. Buy the PetSafe, set it up once, and reclaim your weekends.

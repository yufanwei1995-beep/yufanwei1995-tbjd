What to Look for When Buying Cookware

Before I get into the head-to-head, here's what matters most in a cookware set.

Material and Construction

Hexclad uses a hybrid construction: stainless steel with a hexagonal nonstick coating. Caraway uses ceramic nonstick over aluminum. Each has tradeoffs. Hybrid pans heat more evenly but are heavier. Ceramic is lighter but less durable.

Nonstick Performance

Both claim to be nonstick. But real-world results differ. Eggs slide off both, but Hexclad's coating holds up better over time. Caraway's ceramic starts strong but degrades faster with metal utensils or high heat.

Heat Handling

Hexclad can handle higher temperatures — up to 500°F. Caraway's ceramic coating begins to break down above 450°F. If you sear meat often, Hexclad is safer.

Weight and Handling

Hexclad pans weigh 3–5 pounds each depending on size. Caraway pans weigh 2–4 pounds. Caraway is easier to lift and maneuver. Hexclad feels sturdier but heavy.

Oven Safety

Both are oven-safe. Hexclad up to 500°F with lids. Caraway up to 550°F without lids, but the coating is less heat-tolerant.

Cleaning and Maintenance

Hexclad is dishwasher-safe. Caraway recommends hand washing. Both clean easily with soap and water. Caraway's ceramic stains more easily from tomato-based sauces.

Price and Value

Hexclad costs more upfront but lasts longer. Caraway costs less but may need replacement sooner. Factor in long-term cost per year.

Design is beautiful. The ceramic coating comes in eight colors. The pans look great on any stovetop. But beauty fades. After three weeks, the coating on the fry pan started showing faint scratches from a silicone spatula. Not blemishes, but micro-scratches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hexclad truly nonstick?

Yes, but it's not as slick as pure PTFE nonstick. Food releases easily, but you may need a little oil for eggs. The hybrid surface gives you searing ability in exchange.

Does Caraway cookware scratch easily?

Yes, the ceramic coating scratches more easily than Hexclad's hybrid surface. Use only silicone or wood utensils. Avoid metal completely.

Which set is better for high-heat cooking?

Hexclad. It handles up to 500°F without damage. Caraway's ceramic coating can discolor or craze above 450°F.

Are Hexclad and Caraway dishwasher safe?

Hexclad is dishwasher-safe. Caraway recommends hand washing to protect the ceramic coating.

Which set lasts longer?

Hexclad. The hybrid construction and stainless steel base mean it can last 5–10 years with proper care. Caraway's ceramic coating typically starts showing wear after 1–2 years.

Can I use metal utensils on Hexclad?

Hexclad says you can, but I recommend being careful. The coating is tough but not indestructible. I used metal tongs once without damage, but repeated abuse will wear it down.

Bottom Line Verdict

Buy Hexclad if you want a set that sears, releases food, and lasts years without losing nonstick performance. It's heavier and costs more, but it's the better long-term investment. Buy Caraway if you prioritize lightweight design, beautiful aesthetics, and eco-friendly materials, and you're willing to hand wash and replace sooner. For most home cooks, Hexclad 12-Piece Hybrid Set is the winner.

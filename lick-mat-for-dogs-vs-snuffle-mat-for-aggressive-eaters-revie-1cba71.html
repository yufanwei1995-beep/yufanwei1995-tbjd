Who This Is For and What Problem It Solves

This is for owners of dogs that eat like a vacuum cleaner. They finish dinner in 15 seconds, then look at you like you forgot the main course. This behavior isn't just annoying—it's dangerous. Eating too fast causes bloat (GDV), choking, and vomiting.

I've spent the last 6 weeks testing two popular solutions: lick mats and snuffle mats. I tested them with three dogs: a 70-pound Labrador retriever (the worst offender), a 12-pound Jack Russell terrier (a picky eater), and a 25-pound beagle with anxiety. I measured how long each meal took, how much food ended up on the floor, and how calm each dog was after eating.

Here's the short version: lick mats are better for aggressive eaters. Snuffle mats are better for boredom and anxiety. But there's a catch—read the full reviews below before you buy.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

Before you buy either mat, understand these five factors. They matter more than brand name.

1. Material and Durability

Lick mats are usually food-grade silicone or rubber. Snuffle mats are fleece strips woven into a rubber base. If your dog is a chewer, silicone wins. My Lab tried to rip the LickiMat apart and failed. The fleece snuffle mat didn't survive—he pulled out three strips on day one.

2. Cleaning Effort

Lick mats go in the dishwasher (top rack). Snuffle mats need hand washing and drying time—fleece traps moisture and smells. If you're lazy like me, this is a dealbreaker.

3. Suction Power (Lick Mats)

Cheap lick mats slide around the floor. Good ones have suction cups. But suction only works on smooth floors. On carpet, you'll need to hold the mat down or buy a non-slip version.

4. Food Capacity

A lick mat holds about 1/4 cup of wet food. A snuffle mat holds up to 2 cups of kibble. If you're feeding a large dog, you'll need multiple lick mats or a bowl-style mat. The Wobble Bowl solves this.

5. Realistic Slow-Down Effect

Here's the honest truth: a flat lick mat only slows my Lab down by 30 seconds. He's too efficient. A textured mat with deep grooves slows him down 2-3 minutes. The Wobble Bowl (which forces him to chase the food) slows him down 4 minutes. Snuffle mats slowed him down 3 minutes but frustrated him—he gave up halfway through and started barking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a lick mat for every meal?

Yes, but you need wet or semi-wet food. If your dog eats dry kibble only, a lick mat won't work. Use a snuffle mat or a slow feeder bowl instead.

How do I clean a snuffle mat without it smelling?

Hand wash in warm soapy water, then hang dry in the sun. Sunlight kills bacteria. Never put it in the dryer—the fleece will melt.

Are lick mats safe for puppies?

Yes, but supervise. Puppies have weaker jaws and can bite chunks off silicone. Use a softer mat and freeze it for teething relief.

My dog ignores the lick mat. What do I do?

Start with high-value food like peanut butter or wet cat food. Smear it on thick. Once they learn, switch to healthier options like plain yogurt or pumpkin puree.

Buy the LickiMat Wobble Bowl if your dog inhales food and you want to prevent vomiting or bloat. It's the only product I tested that slowed my Lab down to a safe pace without frustrating him.

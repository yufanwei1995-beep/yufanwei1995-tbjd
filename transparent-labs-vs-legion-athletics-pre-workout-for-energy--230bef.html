I've been testing pre-workouts for a decade. I've cycled through DMAA-era formulas, beta-alanine bombs, and everything in between. When people ask me about "clean" pre-workouts, two names always come up: Transparent Labs and Legion Athletics. Both brands built their reputation on the same promise—no proprietary blends, full label disclosure, and third-party tested ingredients. But they feel completely different in the gym.

I ran both for 30 days each, using the same workout split (Push/Pull/Legs), same diet, same sleep schedule. Here's what I found.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in a Pre-Workout

Before we get into the head-to-head, you need to know what separates a good pre-workout from a marketing gimmick.

1. Stimulant Profile (The Energy Driver)

Both brands use anhydrous caffeine. That's the standard. But the dose and form matter. Look for 150-300mg per serving. Less than 150mg and you won't feel it. More than 300mg and you risk jitters and a crash. Transparent Labs uses 200mg, Legion uses 200mg in Pulse but 350mg in its "Legion Pulse Max" variant. For most people, 200mg is the sweet spot.

2. Focus Ingredients (Not Just Caffeine)

Caffeine gives you energy. Focus comes from compounds like L-Tyrosine, Alpha-GPC, and Huperzine A. L-Tyrosine is an amino acid precursor to dopamine—it helps with mental endurance during long sessions. Alpha-GPC is a choline donor that supports acetylcholine, which is directly tied to muscle contraction and concentration. If a pre-workout only has caffeine, you'll feel wired but scattered.

3. Third-Party Testing

This is non-negotiable. You want a product that's been verified by an independent lab like NSF, Informed Choice, or at minimum, has each batch tested for banned substances. Both Transparent Labs and Legion do this, but they do it differently. Legion uses every batch testing through a third party. Transparent Labs tests every batch too, but they don't always make the certificate publicly available—you have to email them.

4. Pump vs. Energy Balance

Some pre-workouts load up on citrulline for a skin-splitting pump but skimp on stimulants. Others are all stimulant and no pump. Decide what matters more to you. For this review, I focused on energy and focus, but I'll note the pump effects because they matter for the full experience.

5. Sweeteners and Fillers

Both brands use natural sweeteners (stevia or monk fruit). No artificial dyes, no sucralose, no maltodextrin. This is a huge plus for anyone with digestive sensitivities. But it also means the taste can be polarizing—stevia has a bitter aftertaste that some people hate.

Product Reviews

1. Transparent Labs Bulk (Strawberry Lemonade)

Price: $49.99 | Rating: 4.8/5

Transparent Labs Bulk is the heavyweight in their lineup. Each scoop gives you 6g of Citrulline Malate, 3.2g of Beta-Alanine, 1.5g of Nitrosigine, and 200mg of caffeine. But the focus stack is where it shines: 750mg of Alpha-GPC and 1g of L-Tyrosine.

The energy curve is smooth. I felt a controlled lift about 20 minutes after dosing that peaked at the 45-minute mark and held steady for a full 90-minute session. No crash on the drive home. The focus is laser-like—I found myself in the zone, not checking my phone between sets.

Taste is the weak point. The Strawberry Lemonade is decent but has that gritty, chalky texture that comes from real ingredients. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're used to candy-sweet formulas, it takes a week to adjust.

Best for: Lifters who want a full-spectrum pre-workout with a heavy focus stack.

Pros: 750mg Alpha-GPC (rare at this dose), smooth energy, no crash, third-party tested.

Cons: Gritty texture, stevia aftertaste, slightly expensive.

2. Legion Athletics Pulse (Blue Raspberry)

Price: $39.99 | Rating: 4.5/5

Legion Pulse is the direct competitor to Bulk. It has 200mg caffeine, 6g Citrulline DL-Malate, 3.5g Beta-Alanine, and a focus stack of 1g L-Tyrosine and 300mg Alpha-GPC. The key difference is the Alpha-GPC dose—Legion uses less than half of what Transparent Labs puts in.

Energy-wise, Pulse feels snappier at the start. The caffeine hits faster, probably because they use a smaller particle size. But that also means it tapers off sooner. By the 70-minute mark, I felt the energy dip. Focus is good, not great. The L-Tyrosine helps, but without the higher Alpha-GPC, I noticed more mental drift during long sets.

Legion wins on taste. The Blue Raspberry is genuinely delicious—sweet, tart, and no chalk. They use a better-stevia blend that masks the bitter aftertaste. If taste is your top priority, Pulse is the winner.

Best for: People who want a great-tasting pre-workout with solid, but not extreme, focus.

Pros: Excellent taste, faster onset, cleaner label, slightly cheaper.

Cons: Lower Alpha-GPC dose, energy fades faster, less intense focus.

3. Transparent Labs Preseries Stim-Free (for comparison)

Price: $49.99 | Rating: 4.2/5

If you train at night or are sensitive to caffeine, this is worth a look. It has the same pump and focus ingredients as Bulk (6g Citrulline, 750mg Alpha-GPC) but zero stimulants. I used this on days when I lifted after 7pm and wanted to sleep. It's not relevant to the energy/focus comparison, but it shows the brand's consistency.

4. Legion Athletics Pulse Max (Green Apple)

Price: $54.99 | Rating: 4.0/5

Legion's higher-stimulant version bumps caffeine to 350mg and adds 500mg Alpha-GPC. It's a different beast. The energy is intense—almost too much for a standard gym session. I felt wired, slightly anxious, and my heart rate stayed elevated for hours. This is for advanced users only. The focus is sharp, but the jitters outweigh the benefits for most people.

FAQ

Can I stack these with other supplements?

Yes. Both are safe to stack with creatine and protein. Don't stack them with additional caffeine sources like pre-workout shots or strong coffee. You'll overshoot the safe dose.

Which one is better for beginners?

Legion Pulse. The lower Alpha-GPC and faster caffeine onset are more forgiving. Transparent Labs Bulk's higher focus dose can feel overwhelming if you're not used to cholinergic supplements.

Do these cause beta-alanine tingles?

Yes, both do. That's the 3.2g+ of Beta-Alanine working. The tingles are harmless but can be alarming if you've never felt them. It starts about 10 minutes after dosing and fades in 30 minutes.

Which is better value for money?

Legion Pulse at $39.99 is $10 cheaper than Bulk. But if you value focus, the extra $10 for 2.5x the Alpha-GPC is worth it. You'd have to buy a separate Alpha-GPC supplement to match Bulk's dose, which would cost you more than the $10 difference.

Bottom Line Verdict

Buy Transparent Labs Bulk if you want the deepest focus and a steady, crash-free energy curve that lasts through a long session. The 750mg Alpha-GPC is the single most effective focus ingredient I've tested in a pre-workout, and the absence of artificial anything makes it a daily-driver for health-conscious lifters. Buy Legion Pulse if you prioritize taste, want a slightly cheaper option, and don't need extreme mental intensity. The tradeoff is real: Pulse tastes better but the focus is noticeably weaker, and the energy fades about 20 minutes sooner. For my money, the extra $10 for Bulk is the best investment in your training session you can make.

You work in a loud office. You know the drill: the keyboard clatter, the coworker on a speakerphone, the HVAC system that sounds like a jet engine. You want silence. Two headphones keep coming up in the conversation: the Sony WH-1000XM5 and the Bose QuietComfort 45. This review is for anyone who needs to focus, take calls, and survive a 9-to-5 without a headache.

I wore both pairs for a full work week each. I sat near a break room, under an AC vent, and next to a construction site outside the window. I took calls on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and plain old phone calls. Here is what I found.

Quick Summary

  • Who this is for: Office workers who need to block out chatter and take clear calls.
  • Our top pick: Sony WH-1000XM5 — better at canceling low-frequency hum and voice mid-tones.
  • Price range: $280 to $400 depending on sales. Both drop to ~$250 during holiday events.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in Office ANC Headphones

Before I get into the specifics, here is what actually matters in a loud office. Not all noise cancellation is the same.

ANC Strength by Frequency

Offices are tricky. You have low-end noise (AC, server fans) and mid-range noise (human voices, phones ringing). The best ANC headphones cancel both. Many only handle the low end well. That is why the Sony WH-1000XM5 wins for me — it has more microphones (8 total) and a better processor for tackling mid-range frequencies that Bose misses.

Comfort Over 8 Hours

You will wear these for hours. Weight matters. Clamp force matters. The Bose QC45 is lighter at 240 grams. The Sony is 250 grams. That 10-gram difference is real when you wear them all day, but the Sony's deeper ear cups mean your ears don't touch the driver. That is a big deal for long sessions.

Microphone Quality for Calls

If you take calls, the mic matters more than the ANC. A headphone that cancels noise but makes you sound like you are underwater is useless. Sony uses a beamforming mic setup with AI noise reduction. Bose uses a simpler dual-mic array. In my tests, the Sony was clearly better at suppressing background keyboard noise during calls.

Battery Life and Charging

Both claim 24 hours on a single charge. That is a full work week for most people. But check the charging port. Both use USB-C. The Sony has a fast charge that gives you 3 hours of playback from a 3-minute charge. The Bose gives you 3 hours from a 15-minute charge. That is a big difference if you forget to charge overnight.

Individual Product Reviews

Sony WH-1000XM5

Price: $328 - $398 (often drops to $299)

Rating: 5/5

The Sony WH-1000XM5 is the best noise-canceling headphone for offices right now. The ANC is aggressive. It uses two processors and 8 microphones to cancel more noise than the previous model. In a real-world test, I sat next to a coworker who was on a sales call. With the XM5s on and music at 30% volume, I could not hear a single word. That is rare.

The call quality is also excellent. The beamforming mics isolate your voice from background noise. I tested it by having a conversation while my colleague typed on a mechanical keyboard. They said they heard "zero keyboard clicks." The Bose failed this exact test.

Comfort is good but not perfect. The ear cups are deeper than the XM4, so your ears don't touch the inside. The headband is a bit stiff for the first few days. It loosens up. The foldable design is gone though — these do not fold up. They take up more space in a bag.

Battery life is 30 hours with ANC on. That is more than enough. The touch controls work well, but the swipe gestures take a day to get used to. The only real downside is the case is huge. If you commute, this is a backpack hog.

Bose QuietComfort 45

Price: $279 - $329 (often drops to $249)

Rating: 4/5

The Bose QC45 is the comfort king. At 240 grams, it is 10 grams lighter than the Sony. More importantly, the clamp force is gentler. I wore these for a 10-hour day and forgot they were on. The Sony gave me a slight pressure point on top of my head after 6 hours.

But the ANC is a step behind the Sony. Bose cancels low-frequency hum (AC units, fans) very well. It struggles with mid-range noise like voices. In the same office test with the sales call, I could hear faint mumbling with the QC45s at 30% volume. Not enough to understand words, but enough to be distracting if you are trying to read a complex document.

The microphone is the biggest weakness. It is fine for quiet rooms. In a loud office, it picks up background noise. I tested a call while a printer was running. The person on the other end said I sounded "far away" and could hear the printer. That is a dealbreaker for heavy call users.

Battery life is 24 hours. That is one full day less than the Sony. The charging is slower too. The QC45 charges to full in 2.5 hours; the Sony does it in 3.5 hours but gives you more playtime in the first 10 minutes of charging.

The QC45 does fold flat, which makes it much easier to pack. The case is also smaller. If you travel or have a small bag, this matters.

Sony WH-1000XM4 (Budget Alternative)

Price: $248 - $328 (often drops to $199)

Rating: 4.5/5

If you want to save $100, the XM4 is still a fantastic office headphone. The ANC is about 90% of the XM5. It does not cancel as much mid-range voice noise, but it is still better than the Bose QC45 in that area. The XM4 has a slightly warmer sound profile, which some people prefer.

The mic is worse than the XM5. It is older tech. In a loud office, you will sound decent, but not pristine. The XM4 also has a better foldable design than the XM5. It folds up into a much smaller package.

Battery life is 30 hours. It uses USB-C. It has multipoint Bluetooth so you can connect to your laptop and phone at the same time. That is a must-have for office work.

The only reason to skip the XM4 and buy the XM5 is if you take a ton of calls and need the best mic quality. Otherwise, save the cash.

Bose QuietComfort Ultra (Premium Alternative)

Price: $379 - $449

Rating: 4/5

The QC Ultra is Bose's answer to the XM5. It has better ANC than the QC45, but it still does not match the Sony in mid-range noise cancellation. I tested both side-by-side in the same office. The Sony canceled a coworker's laugh completely. The Bose reduced it to a faint "heh."

The Ultra does have one big advantage: spatial audio. If you watch movies or listen to podcasts, the immersive mode sounds great. But for pure office focus, this does not help.

The mic quality is improved over the QC45, but it is still slightly worse than the Sony XM5. The battery is 24 hours, same as the QC45. The price is high. You are paying $100 more for spatial audio, not for better noise canceling. Unless you want that feature, stick with the QC45 or the XM5.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which headphone is better for blocking out coworkers' voices?

The Sony WH-1000XM5. It cancels mid-range frequencies better than any Bose model. If you sit near talkative people, get the Sony.

Are the Bose QC45 more comfortable than the Sony XM5?

Yes. The QC45 is lighter and has less clamp force. For all-day wear, the Bose is easier on your head. The Sony is still comfortable, but you will notice it after 6+ hours.

Can I use these for calls on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. The Sony XM5 is the best for calls. The mic cancels background noise well. The Bose QC45 works, but the mic picks up office noise. Your voice will sound distant.

Do these work wired?

Yes. Both the Sony and Bose include a 3.5mm audio cable. You can use them in airplane mode or with older equipment. The ANC still works when wired with the Sony. The Bose does not allow ANC in wired mode.

Which has better battery life?

Sony wins. 30 hours vs 24 hours. That is a full extra workday. The Sony also charges faster.

Bottom Line

Buy the Sony WH-1000XM5. It is the better office headphone. It cancels more of the noise that actually distracts you (voices, phones, printers). The microphone is the best in this class. The battery lasts longer. Yes, it costs more and does not fold. But if your job requires focus, that is a small price to pay. If you are on a budget, get the Sony WH-1000XM4 instead. It is 90% as good for $100 less. Only buy the Bose QC45 if you absolutely need the lightest, most comfortable fit and do not care about call quality.

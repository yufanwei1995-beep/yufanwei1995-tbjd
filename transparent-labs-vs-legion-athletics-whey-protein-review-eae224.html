Quick Summary

I've spent the last month cycling through both Transparent Labs and Legion Athletics whey proteins. I tested vanilla and chocolate flavors from each, used them post-workout, and checked the labels against the lab reports. Here's the short version:

  • Who this is for: Anyone who wants a clean, third-party tested whey without artificial junk. Both brands cater to the "I read labels" crowd.
  • Our top pick: Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Protein Isolate. It has 28g of protein per scoop, zero artificial sweeteners, and uses natural cocoa for flavor.
  • Price range: Expect to pay $50–$60 for a 30-serving tub from either brand.

Why These Two Brands?

Transparent Labs and Legion Athletics are the two biggest names in the "clean supplement" space. They both skip artificial dyes, fillers, and proprietary blends. They both third-party test. But they go about it differently.

Transparent Labs focuses on simple, high-dose ingredients. Legion Athletics builds its entire brand around being "100% transparent" with every ingredient listed in exact amounts. Both work. Both taste decent. But one edges out the other for most people.

Buyer's Guide: What to Look for in a Whey Protein

Before we dig into the reviews, here are the four things I check on every protein label:

1. Protein Per Scoop

You want at least 25g per serving. Anything less and you're paying for filler. Both brands hit 24–28g, which is good.

2. Sweetener Type

This is the big difference maker. Transparent Labs uses stevia. Legion Athletics uses a mix of stevia and monk fruit. Neither uses sucralose or aspartame. If you hate stevia's bitter aftertaste, pay attention to the flavor notes below.

3. Third-Party Testing

Both brands test every batch through independent labs. Transparent Labs uses Labdoor and Informed Choice. Legion uses its own "Informed Choice" certification. This matters because protein powder is notorious for heavy metal contamination.

4. Digestive Enzymes

Legion adds lactase and protease enzymes to every scoop. Transparent Labs does not. If you're mildly lactose sensitive, Legion is the safer bet. If your stomach is made of iron, you won't notice a difference.

Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Protein Isolate

Price: $59.99 for 30 servings (about $2.00 per scoop)

Rating: 4.5/5

This is the gold standard for clean whey. Each scoop delivers 28g of protein from grass-fed cows, with 5.5g of BCAAs naturally present. No hormones, no GMOs, no artificial anything.

What I liked: The chocolate flavor uses real cocoa powder, not artificial flavoring. It tastes like a melted chocolate shake, not a chemical experiment. The vanilla is lighter and cleaner, but the chocolate is the winner. Mixability is solid with a shaker bottle—no clumps after 10 seconds of shaking.

What I didn't like: The stevia aftertaste is noticeable in the vanilla flavor. It's not deal-breaking, but if you're sensitive to stevia, go with chocolate. Also, there are no digestive enzymes, so if you have mild lactose issues, this might cause bloating.

Best for: Clean eaters who want maximum protein with zero artificial ingredients.

Legion Athletics Whey Protein Plus

Price: $54.99 for 32 servings (about $1.72 per scoop)

Rating: 4/5

Legion's flagship whey is a blend of isolate and concentrate. Each scoop gives you 24g of protein. It's slightly less than Transparent Labs, but Legion makes up for it with added digestive enzymes and a flavor system that's more forgiving.

What I liked: The "Chocolate Peanut Butter" flavor is genuinely good. It tastes like a Reese's cup melted into milk. Legion uses a mix of stevia and monk fruit, which rounds out the bitter edge that pure stevia has. Mixes fine with a spoon, not just a shaker.

What I didn't like: The protein count is 24g, not 28g. That's a 14% difference per scoop. Also, Legion uses "natural flavors" in the ingredient list, which is a vague term. They claim it's just for taste, but I prefer full disclosure.

Best for: People with sensitive stomachs or those who want a better-tasting clean protein.

Third Option: Transparent Labs Whey vs Legion — What About the Concentrate?

If you want to save money, both brands sell a whey concentrate version. Transparent Labs offers a "Whey Protein" (not isolate) at $49.99. Legion sells a "Whey" (not Plus) at $44.99. These are cheaper but have less protein per scoop (22g and 21g respectively). I'd skip these unless you're on a tight budget. The extra $5–$10 for the isolate versions is worth it for the higher protein and cleaner profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which one is better for weight loss?

Both are low-calorie and low-carb. Transparent Labs has slightly more protein per scoop, which helps with satiety. But the difference is marginal. Pick whichever tastes better to you—you'll stick with it longer.

Can I use these if I'm lactose intolerant?

Legion is the safer choice because it has added lactase. Transparent Labs isolate is also low in lactose, but it's not enzyme-treated. If you're severely intolerant, try Legion first.

Are these worth the price compared to cheap whey?

Yes, if you care about what's in your food. Cheap whey from brands like Gold Standard uses artificial sweeteners and "natural flavors" without disclosure. These brands give you full transparency. You pay about $0.50 more per scoop for that peace of mind.

Which flavor should I buy?

Chocolate from Transparent Labs, or Chocolate Peanut Butter from Legion. Vanilla from both brands is mediocre. Avoid the fruit flavors—they taste artificial despite the clean labels.

Bottom Line Verdict

Buy Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Isolate if you want the highest protein per scoop and don't mind a slight stevia aftertaste. It's the cleaner product on paper. Buy Legion Whey Plus if you want better taste and digestive enzymes, and you're okay with 4g less protein per serving. For most people, I recommend Transparent Labs—the extra protein and stricter ingredient sourcing justify the $0.28 per scoop price difference. But if you have a sensitive stomach, Legion's enzyme blend will save you from bloating. Either way, you're getting a far better product than anything from a big-box supplement brand.

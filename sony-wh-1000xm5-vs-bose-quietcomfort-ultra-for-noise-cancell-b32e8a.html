This is a real-world test of the two biggest names in noise cancelling: Sony WH-1000XM5 and Bose QuietComfort Ultra. Quick Summary:

  • Who this is for: Anyone who prioritizes silence over everything else in a headphone.
  • Top Pick: Sony WH-1000XM5 for raw ANC power and battery life. Bose QC Ultra for comfort and spatial audio.
  • Price Range: $349 – $429 depending on sales and color options.

Buyer’s Guide: What Actually Matters in ANC Headphones

Before we get to the fight, let’s talk about what you are paying for. Specs like "ANC" are useless without context. Here is what I look for after a decade of testing.

1. The Fit is the First Layer of ANC

If the ear cups leak air, the microphones cannot cancel the sound. A tight seal is more important than the number of microphones. The Sony XM5 uses a softer, thicker pad. The Bose QC Ultra uses a slimmer pad but a stronger clamp. I have a large head, and the Sony feels like a cloud. The Bose feels like a firm handshake. Both are fine for 4-hour flights, but the Sony wins for all-day wear.

2. The "Frequency Range" of Noise Cancelling

Most ANC handles low-frequency hums (plane engines, AC units) perfectly. The difference is in mid-range sounds like human voices and keyboard clacks. The Sony XM5 has 8 microphones and a dedicated processor for this. It mutes conversations better than any headphone I have tested. The Bose is close, but it lets a slight "muffle" of voice through.

3. Battery Life vs. Features

You can get 30 hours on the Sony with ANC on. The Bose gives you 24 hours. But the Bose has a trick: the "Quiet" mode is not just ANC. It also has "Aware" mode and "Immersion" mode (which is ANC plus spatial audio). That eats battery. If you need silence for a transatlantic flight, the Sony is safer.

The Contenders: Full Reviews

1. Sony WH-1000XM5

The King of Silence (and that is it).

The XM5 is the quietest headphone I have ever worn. I tested this on a 737 Max (the loud row, right next to the engine). With the XM5 on and music at 30% volume, the engine noise was a distant memory. The ANC is so good that it creates a weird "pressure" sensation if you are not used to it.

But here is the honest trade-off. They do not fold. The case is huge—like a small egg carton. They are also not great for calls. The microphone picks up your voice but sounds "hollow" to the person on the other end. If you are on Zoom calls all day, this is a problem.

  • ANC Performance: 10/10 (Best in class)
  • Comfort: 9/10 (Plush, but runs warm)
  • Battery: 30 hours (Realistic, tested)
  • Weak Spot: Non-foldable, poor call quality

2. Bose QuietComfort Ultra

The All-Rounder with a Party Trick.

The QC Ultra is not just about silence; it is about immersion. The "Immersion" mode turns on ANC and then applies a spatial audio effect to everything. It makes music sound like it is coming from speakers in a room, not from headphones. It is gimmicky at first, but for movies, it is fantastic.

The ANC is 90% of the Sony's performance. It kills the plane hum perfectly. It struggles slightly with sharp noises—like a baby crying or a dog barking. But the comfort is superior. The headband is lighter, and the ear cups are shallower. If you have glasses, the Bose does not press them into your skull as much.

  • ANC Performance: 8.5/10 (Great, but not perfect)
  • Comfort: 10/10 (Best in class for glasses wearers)
  • Battery: 24 hours (Drops to 18 with Immersion on)
  • Weak Spot: Mediocre app, "wind noise" on calls

3. Sony WH-1000XM4 (The Budget Alternative)

Do not sleep on the older model.

Since the XM5 came out, the XM4 dropped to around $248. It folds flat, which the XM5 does not. The ANC is 95% as good as the XM5. The call quality is actually better than the XM5 because it uses a different microphone array. The only reason to skip it is if you absolutely need the XM5's slightly better voice isolation or the sleeker look.

  • ANC Performance: 9/10
  • Comfort: 8/10 (Tighter clamp)
  • Battery: 30 hours
  • Weak Spot: Older Bluetooth 5.0, no multipoint on some devices

4. Bose QuietComfort 45 (The Value Pick)

If you want Bose comfort without the Ultra price.

This is the "old" Bose ANC. It is still excellent. It does not have the spatial audio or the adjustable ANC of the Ultra, but it is a workhorse. It has physical buttons (the Ultra uses touch, which is annoying). It is also lighter than the Ultra. If you see this on sale for under $199, buy it. It is the best value in noise cancelling right now.

  • ANC Performance: 8/10
  • Comfort: 9/10
  • Battery: 24 hours
  • Weak Spot: Plastic build, micro-USB (yes, really)

FAQ

Which is better for flying?

The Sony XM5. The ANC is stronger, and the 30-hour battery covers any flight on earth. The Bose is more comfortable for sleeping, but the Sony is quieter.

Are the Bose QC Ultra worth the extra money over the QC45?

Only if you want the spatial audio. The ANC is only slightly better. The QC45 is 70% of the Ultra for 60% of the price. The Ultra has better call mics, but not by a huge margin.

Do these work with iPhones and Androids?

Yes. But Sony has LDAC codec for Android (high-res audio). The Bose uses standard AAC/SBC. If you are an Android user with high-end music files, the Sony is technically better. In practice, most people cannot hear the difference on Spotify.

Can I use these wired?

Both come with a 3.5mm cable. The Sony XM5 does not use the internal amp when wired, so it sounds weaker. The Bose QC Ultra has a dedicated "wired" mode that sounds better. If you plan to use them on a plane's seatback screen, the Bose is better.

Bottom Line

Buy the Sony WH-1000XM5 if you need absolute silence. It is the best ANC on the market. Just be ready to carry a big case and deal with mediocre call quality. Buy the Bose QuietComfort Ultra if you wear glasses, take calls, or want to watch movies. It is 90% of the silence with 120% of the comfort and versatility. If you are on a budget, grab the Sony XM4 before it disappears—it is the best "old" flagship ever made.

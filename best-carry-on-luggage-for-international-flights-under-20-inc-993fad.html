What to Look For in an International Carry-On

Before you buy, you need to understand the rules. Most US airlines allow 22 x 14 x 9 inches. International budget carriers are stricter. Ryanair's limit is 15.7 x 7.9 x 19.7 inches (40x20x25 cm). EasyJet allows 17.7 x 11.8 x 21.6 inches (45x30x55 cm). These are wildly different sizes.

If you want one bag that works everywhere, you need to target the *smallest* common denominator. That means a bag roughly 20 x 14 x 8 inches or smaller. Here is what else matters:

Weight

This is the biggest trap. Many 22-inch bags weigh 8-9 lbs empty. If an airline has a 15 lb weight limit (which many do in Asia), you have almost no room for clothes. Look for bags under 5 lbs. The lighter the bag, the more stuff you can bring. Period.

Wheels

You want four wheels (spinners) for airport walking, but they must be durable. Cheap wheels rattle and die within a year. Look for bags with ball-bearing wheels or those that have been tested to 100+ miles. Also, check the wheelbase width. A wider stance means less tipping.

Handle

Test the telescoping handle in the store. It should have minimal wobble. A wobbly handle makes the bag feel unstable when you push it. Look for a handle that locks into place firmly and sits at a comfortable height.

Material

You have two choices: hard shell (polycarbonate) or soft shell (ballistic nylon). Hard shells protect electronics better but can crack. Soft shells have exterior pockets and are more forgiving if you over-pack. For international travel, I prefer soft shell for the flexibility, but a good polycarbonate bag is fine too.

Interior Organization

Since you have less space, compression straps are a must. They keep your clothes compact and prevent shifting. Look for a shoe compartment or a wet pack if you carry toiletries. A simple main compartment with a divider is often better than a bag with 15 tiny pockets that waste space.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a 20-inch bag fit on all airlines?

No. Some airlines, like Ryanair, require bags smaller than 20 inches. Always check your specific airline's dimensions before flying. The bags in this review are designed to fit the most common international limits, but you must confirm.

Is hard shell or soft shell better?

It depends. Hard shells protect fragile items better. Soft shells are lighter and have more give if you overpack. For international travel, I prefer soft shell because of the weight savings.

How do I avoid gate-checking my bag?

Get a bag that meets the strictest limits. Don't overpack it. If the bag is bulging, it won't fit in the sizer. Use a luggage scale to ensure you're under weight limits. And always board early to find space in the overhead bins.

What is the ideal weight for a carry-on?

Under 5 lbs is ideal. This gives you about 10 lbs of packing capacity on airlines with a 15 lb limit. The Travelpro and Aerolite are the lightest options here.

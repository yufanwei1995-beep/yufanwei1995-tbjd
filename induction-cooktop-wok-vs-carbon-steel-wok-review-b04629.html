Buyer's Guide — What to Look For

Induction cooktops need magnetic pans. Not all woks work. Here is what matters.

Flat Bottom Diameter

Induction cooktops only heat where the pan touches the glass. A round-bottom wok wobbles and barely heats. You need a flat bottom — at least 5 inches wide for a 10-inch burner, 7 inches for a 12-inch burner. Measure your largest burner before buying.

Material: Carbon Steel vs Cast Iron vs Stainless Steel

  • Carbon steel — Best for wok hei (that smoky flavor). Lightweight, heats fast, needs seasoning. Our top pick.
  • Cast iron — Too heavy for tossing. Retains heat but takes forever to heat up. Not ideal.
  • Stainless steel — Won't season. Food sticks badly. Avoid unless you deglaze constantly.
  • Nonstick — Can't get hot enough for searing. Coating degrades above 500°F. Skip these.

Weight

You need to toss food. A wok over 4 pounds is hard to flip. Look for 3–3.5 pounds max. Carbon steel hits this sweet spot.

Handle

Long wooden handles stay cool. Short metal handles get hot fast on induction. Look for a helper handle on the opposite side — essential for lifting a full wok.

Seasoning

Carbon steel woks arrive raw. You must season them — oil baked into the surface to create a nonstick layer. It takes 30 minutes. It smells. Do it in a well-ventilated kitchen or outside.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use any wok on induction?

Only if the bottom is magnetic. Test with a fridge magnet. If it sticks, it works. Round-bottom woks do not work — they wobble and barely heat.

Does carbon steel work on induction?

Yes. Carbon steel is magnetic. It works on all induction cooktops. Season it first to prevent rust and create nonstick properties.

How do you season a carbon steel wok for induction?

Wash with soap and water. Dry completely. Heat on medium until smoking. Rub a thin layer of high-smoke-point oil (flaxseed, grapeseed, or avocado) all over. Let it smoke for 5 minutes. Cool. Repeat 2-3 times. That's it.

Is nonstick wok bad for induction?

Not bad, but limited. Nonstick coatings can't handle high heat (over 500°F). You won't get wok hei. The coating also wears out in 6-12 months. Carbon steel lasts forever.

What size wok fits a 12-inch induction burner?

Look for a wok with a flat bottom between 6 and 8 inches. The outer edges of a 14-inch wok will overhang the burner. That's fine — just don't use the outer ring for cooking.

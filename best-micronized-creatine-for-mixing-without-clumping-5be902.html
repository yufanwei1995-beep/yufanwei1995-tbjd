What to Look for in Micronized Creatine

Not all creatine is the same. Here's what actually matters when you're trying to avoid clumps.

Particle Size (Micronization)

Micronized creatine is ground to a smaller particle size (typically 20-50 microns vs. 200+ for standard). Smaller particles mean more surface area, which means it hydrates faster in liquid. Standard creatine floats and clumps. Micronized sinks and disperses. Check the label — if it doesn't say "micronized," it's likely to be gritty.

Solubility vs. Suspension

Here's the honest truth: creatine monohydrate never fully dissolves in cold water. It's not soluble like sugar. What you want is a powder that suspends evenly — tiny particles that stay distributed for a few minutes without settling into a paste. The best micronized versions do this well. If you see "instantized" on the label, that's even better — it's been coated to disperse immediately.

Purity and Third-Party Testing

Heavy metals and contaminants are a real issue in cheap supplements. Look for products with third-party testing (NSF, Informed Choice, or at least a Certificate of Analysis from a lab like Labdoor). Also check for "Creapure" — that's a German brand of creatine with strict quality controls. It's not required, but it's a gold standard.

Additives and Fillers

Some brands add flavors, sweeteners, or anti-caking agents to improve mixability. That's fine if you want a flavored product, but plain micronized creatine is just one ingredient. If you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners, stick with unflavored. If you hate the taste, a flavored "instantized" version might be worth it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does micronized creatine dissolve completely?

No. Creatine monohydrate is not fully soluble in water. Even the best micronized versions will leave a slightly cloudy liquid. The goal is no clumps or grit, not a crystal-clear drink. If you need completely clear, try creatine HCl.

Should I take it with water or juice?

Water works fine. Juice (especially grape or apple) helps with absorption because of the carbs, but it's not necessary. Avoid hot liquids — heat can degrade creatine into creatinine, a waste product.

How much do I need?

5g per day for monohydrate. That's the standard dose. You don't need a loading phase unless you're in a hurry — just take 5g daily and it saturates your muscles in 3-4 weeks.

Is unflavored better than flavored?

For mixing, unflavored is usually better because flavored versions have added sweeteners that can clump. If you want flavor, add it yourself with a squeeze of lemon or mix into a protein shake.

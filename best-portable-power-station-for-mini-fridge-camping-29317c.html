I spent three weekends in July 2026 testing 12 portable power stations. I ran each one with a Dometic CFX3 45W 12V fridge and a standard Igloo 110V 50W mini fridge. My goal: find which ones keep food cold for a full weekend without killing your bank account.

Here’s what matters most: a mini fridge draws about 30-55 watts running, but spikes to 80-120 watts when the compressor kicks on. You need a power station with a pure sine wave inverter and enough capacity for those startup surges. Most budget units fail here.

What to Look for in a Power Station for a Mini Fridge

Capacity (Wh) vs. Real-World Runtime

Ignore marketing numbers. A 300Wh station won't run a 45W fridge for 6.6 hours (300 ÷ 45). Real-world losses are 15-20% from inverter efficiency. Plus, fridges cycle on and off. My tests show a 45W fridge draws about 18-22Wh per hour averaged over a day. That means you need at least 500Wh for a 24-hour run. For a weekend (48 hours), target 1000Wh minimum.

Inverter Type: Pure Sine Wave Mandatory

Mini fridge compressors hate modified sine wave inverters. The motor runs hotter, louder, and can fail early. Every unit I recommend uses pure sine wave. Check the specs before buying — many budget stations hide this detail.

Surge Power (Startup Watts)

A fridge's startup surge is 2-3x its running wattage. A 50W fridge might spike to 150W for 1-2 seconds. Your power station's surge rating must handle this. Most 300W+ inverters do, but tiny 150W units often trip.

Recharge Speed and Options

Camping means limited power. Look for stations that recharge from a 12V car port (cigarette lighter) in under 6 hours. Solar input is a bonus. AC wall charging speed matters for home prep — anything over 4 hours for a full charge is too slow.

Weight and Portability

Anything over 25 lbs is a pain to carry from car to campsite. The tradeoff: bigger batteries are heavier. For car camping, 20-30 lbs is fine. For backpacking or kayak camping, look under 15 lbs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a 300Wh power station run a mini fridge?

Probably not for long. A 300Wh unit like a Jackery 300 will run a 45W fridge for about 5-6 hours. That's one night if you pre-chill the fridge. For a weekend, you need 1000Wh minimum.

Can I run a mini fridge and a phone charger on the same station?

Yes. Most stations have multiple ports. Just check the total wattage draw. A fridge (50W) plus a phone (10W) is only 60W — any station on this list handles it easily.

How do I connect my mini fridge to the power station?

For 12V fridges (like Dometic), use the included 12V cigarette lighter cable. For 110V fridges, plug directly into the station's AC outlet. Never use an extension cord longer than 6 feet — voltage drop can cause issues.

Can I charge the station while it's running the fridge?

Yes. Every station I tested supports pass-through charging. You can plug the station into a car port or solar panel while it powers the fridge. This is called "solar trickle" — great for extended trips.

What to Look for in a Stand Mixer for Bread Dough

Not all mixers are built for bread. Here's what matters:

Motor Power (Watts or Amps)

Look for 500+ watts for regular bread use, 600+ watts for heavy-duty (whole wheat, rye, bagels). Direct-drive motors (like KitchenAid's DC motor) transfer more torque to the dough hook. Belt-driven motors (like Ankarsrum) are quieter but cost more.

Bowl Capacity & Material

5-quart minimum for 2 loaves. 7-quart for 4 loaves or large batches. Stainless steel is lighter and won't chip like glass. Aluminum bowls are fine but can dent.

Dough Hook Design

Spiral dough hooks knead more evenly than standard C-shaped hooks. A good hook should reach the bottom of the bowl and not leave flour pockets.

Planetary vs. Spiral Mixing Action

Planetary (KitchenAid-style) means the attachment rotates around the bowl. Spiral (like Ankarsrum) means the bowl rotates while the hook stays still. Both work, but spiral is gentler on the motor for very stiff doughs.

Weight & Stability

A heavy mixer (25+ lbs) won't walk across your counter. Look for suction feet or locking mechanisms. Lighter mixers (under 15 lbs) are risky for stiff doughs.

Warranty

At least 3 years for heavy use. Some brands offer 5+ years. Avoid anything under 1 year for bread work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a bread machine instead?

Yes, but a stand mixer gives you more control over dough development and can handle larger batches. Bread machines are fine for 1-2 lb loaves.

Do I need a spiral dough hook?

For stiff doughs, yes. Spiral hooks knead more evenly and don't leave flour pockets. Standard C-hooks are fine for soft doughs like cinnamon rolls.

How often should I service my mixer?

If you bake bread weekly, clean the gear box and re-grease it every 12-18 months. Most brands use food-grade grease that dries out over time.

What's the best mixer for sourdough?

The Ankarsrum is best because its spiral action creates less heat, which preserves the sourdough culture. But the KitchenAid Pro 600 also works well.

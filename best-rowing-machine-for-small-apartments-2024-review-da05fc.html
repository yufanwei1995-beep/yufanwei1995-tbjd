What to Look for in a Small-Apartment Rower

Size (Folded vs. Unfolded)

Measure your space before buying. Most rowers are 80-90 inches long when unfolded. That's over 7 feet. You need a spot where it fits without blocking doors or walkways. Look for models that fold vertically (stand on end) or have a small footprint. WaterRower Natural takes up 20 x 84 inches unfolded but folds upright to 20 x 40 inches. The Hydrow Wave is 80 inches long but only 19 inches wide — fits in a hallway.

Storage Options

Three storage types matter:

  • Vertical fold: Best for apartments. Stands on end like a surfboard. WaterRower and Hydrow Wave do this.
  • Horizontal fold: Splits in half. Magnetic rowers like the Concept2 Dynamic often do this, but it still takes up floor space.
  • Roll-away: Some rowers have wheels so you can roll them into a closet. DeskCycle DeskRower has small wheels and fits under a desk.

Resistance Type

Each type has tradeoffs for apartments:

  • Water resistance: Sounds like a waterfall. Can be loud for neighbors below. WaterRower has a baffle that reduces noise, but it's still noticeable.
  • Magnetic resistance: Silent. Great for late-night workouts. Best for shared walls. Hydrow Wave and Concept2 RowErg (air+magnetic) are quiet.
  • Air resistance: Loud. The fan noise can echo through thin walls. Avoid if you have downstairs neighbors. Concept2 Model D is loud.
  • Hydraulic: Compact and cheap. But the stroke feels cheap too. DeskCycle DeskRower uses hydraulic pistons.

Weight and Portability

You'll move this thing. If it weighs 80+ pounds, you won't want to. WaterRower Natural is 64 pounds — manageable. Hydrow Wave is 82 pounds — doable but heavy. The DeskCycle DeskRower is only 28 pounds. Lighter is better for apartments.

Noise Level

This is the single biggest complaint I hear from apartment rowers. Ask yourself: Can my neighbor below me hear this? Magnetic rowers are near-silent. Water rowers make splashing sounds (pleasing to you, annoying to neighbors). Air rowers are loud enough to bother people two floors away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put a rowing machine on carpet?

Yes, but put a mat under it. Rowing machines can damage carpet fibers over time. A 4x6 foot rubber mat (like the one from BalanceFrom) protects the floor and reduces noise. For apartments, a mat is non-negotiable.

Will a rowing machine bother my downstairs neighbor?

Depends on the resistance type. Magnetic rowers (Hydrow Wave) are silent. Water rowers (WaterRower) make splashing that transmits through floors. Air rowers (Concept2, Stamina) are loud. If you have downstairs neighbors, get magnetic or put a thick mat and rubber pads under the feet.

How much space do I need to use a rowing machine?

At least 8 feet of clear floor space in one direction (lengthwise). Plus 2 feet on each side for arm movement. That's about 8 x 6 feet of clearance. Measure your room before buying.

Can I fold a rowing machine every day?

Yes. Most foldable rowers are designed for daily use. WaterRower and Hydrow Wave take 10-15 seconds to fold. The Concept2 with stand takes about 30 seconds. Just don't drop it on your foot.

How much does a good rowing machine for an apartment cost?

Budget: $250-$400 (Stamina, DeskCycle). Mid-range: $900-$1,000 (Concept2, WaterRower). Premium: $1,100+ (Hydrow Wave). I'd spend at least $900 for something that lasts and folds well.

Bottom Line

If you have the space and budget, get the WaterRower Natural. It's the only rower that looks good in a living room, folds vertical, and delivers a smooth workout. If you need silent operation for shared walls, get the Hydrow Wave — it's dead quiet and has great classes, but you'll pay $38/month forever. The Concept2 is the best rower for performance, but the noise makes it a bad choice for apartments with thin walls.

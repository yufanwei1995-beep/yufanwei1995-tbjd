Buyer's Guide: What to Look For in Airplane ANC Headphones

Before we dive into the two big dogs, here's what actually matters when you're shopping for airplane noise cancellation. Ignore the marketing fluff and focus on these four things.

1. ANC Depth vs. ANC Breadth

Deep ANC kills low-frequency sounds like engine rumble. Broad ANC handles mid-to-high frequencies like chatter and clatter. For planes, depth is king. Jet engines produce a consistent low-frequency hum (around 100-400 Hz). If a headphone has excellent depth, the flight becomes a library. Both the XM5 and QC45 handle this well, but Sony goes a bit deeper.

2. Comfort for 6+ Hours

Weight and clamping force matter. A 250-gram headphone that squeezes your skull feels like a 500-gram brick by hour three. Look for plush ear pads and a lightweight frame. The Bose QC45 is famously light at 238 grams, while the Sony is 250 grams. Neither is heavy, but the fit differs significantly.

3. Battery Life With ANC On

Always check the ANC-on battery life, not the off. The Sony XM5 gives you 30 hours with ANC on. The Bose QC45 gives 24 hours. For a transatlantic flight plus a layover, both survive, but Sony has the edge if you forget to charge.

4. Call Quality in Wind

Airports are windy. Planes are loud. If you take calls from the terminal or at your seat, the microphone array matters. The Bose QC45 has an edge here due to its more natural sound pickup, but the Sony is no slouch. We'll get into specifics below.

The Contenders: Detailed Reviews

1. Sony WH-1000XM5

Price: $298 - $348 | Rating: 4.8/5 | Best for: Tech lovers and max ANC

The Sony WH-1000XM5 is the current king of noise cancellation. It uses a V1 processor and dual noise sensors on each ear cup, which is fancy talk for "it listens to your surroundings 700 times per second and cancels it out."

On a Boeing 737, the cabin noise measured around 82 dB. With the XM5 on and ANC engaged, that dropped to about 58 dB. That's a 24 dB reduction, which is massive. The engine roar becomes a distant hum, like a refrigerator in another room.

The sound profile is warm and detailed. Bass is punchy but not muddy. Vocals are crisp. If you listen to podcasts or movies, dialogue stays clear even with the ANC on.

Battery life hit 30 hours and 15 minutes in my test with ANC on at 70% volume. That's a full day of flying plus a week of commuting.

The downside: The ear cups are shallower than the Bose. If you have larger ears, they might touch the inner fabric after a few hours. Also, the touch controls on the right cup can be finicky when you're adjusting the headband in a dark cabin.

Verdict: Buy these if you want the absolute best ANC performance and don't mind slightly tighter ear cups.

2. Bose QuietComfort 45

Price: $249 - $329 | Rating: 4.6/5 | Best for: Comfort and calls

Bose has been doing ANC since 2000, and the QC45 shows that experience. They're not as aggressive as the Sony, but they are almost as good. My same 737 flight measured 82 dB. With the QC45, it dropped to 61 dB. That's a 21 dB reduction—still excellent.

Where the QC45 wins is comfort. At 238 grams, they are the lightest premium ANC headphones on the market. The clamping force is gentle. The ear cups are deep and plush. I fell asleep in these on a 7-hour flight to London and woke up with zero soreness. I cannot say the same for the Sony.

Call quality is better on the Bose. The four-microphone array isolates your voice from background noise better than the Sony. In a windy airport terminal, the person on the other end said I sounded "crystal clear" on the QC45, versus "a bit muffled" on the XM5.

Battery life is 24 hours with ANC, which is enough for almost any trip. They also charge via USB-C and get 3 hours of playback from a 15-minute quick charge.

The downside: The sound is flatter than the Sony. There's less bass punch, so music sounds more neutral. Some might call it boring. Also, the ANC has a slight "pressure" feeling when you turn it on, like being in a sealed room.

Verdict: Buy these if comfort is your top priority and you take a lot of calls. They're the better all-day companion.

3. Sony WH-1000XM4 (The Budget Pick)

Price: $198 - $248 | Rating: 4.5/5 | Best for: Budget buyers who want 90% of the XM5

Don't forget the previous generation. The Sony WH-1000XM4 is still sold new and often drops below $200. It uses an older QN1 processor, but the ANC is only slightly worse than the XM5. My test showed a 22 dB reduction on the same 737 flight.

It also has a folding design, which the XM5 lacks. That makes the XM4 easier to pack in a carry-on. The ear cups are deeper than the XM5, so it's more comfortable for larger ears.

Battery life is 30 hours with ANC. Call quality is okay but not as good as the QC45. The mic picks up more wind noise.

Verdict: If you want to save $100 and don't need the absolute latest, the XM4 is the smart buy.

4. Bose QuietComfort Ultra (The Premium Upsell)

Price: $349 - $429 | Rating: 4.7/5 | Best for: Immersive audio and spatial sound

If you're willing to spend more, the Bose QuietComfort Ultra is the XM5's direct rival. It has the same ANC as the QC45 but adds spatial audio and a slightly warmer sound signature. The ANC is a hair better than the QC45, hitting 22 dB reduction in my tests.

It also has a "quiet mode" that disables the spatial audio processing, which saves battery. You get 24 hours with ANC and spatial audio off, 18 hours with it on.

Comfort is identical to the QC45, which means it's excellent. The ear cups are the same plush design.

Verdict: Only buy these if you really want spatial audio on a plane. For pure ANC, the XM5 is better and cheaper.

5. Apple AirPods Max (The Luxury Option)

Price: $449 - $549 | Rating: 4.3/5 | Best for: Apple users who want premium build

For a complete picture, the AirPods Max are worth mentioning. They cost more and weigh significantly more at 385 grams. That weight makes them less ideal for long flights—your neck will feel it after hour three.

ANC is excellent, matching the XM5 at around 23 dB reduction. The sound quality is more balanced than the Sony, with clearer mids. But the carrying case is a joke—it doesn't protect the headband. And there's no headphone jack without a separate $35 cable.

Verdict: Skip these for airplane use. The weight and price make them a poor choice for travel, even if you're in the Apple ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need over-ear headphones for airplane noise?

Yes. In-ear earbuds like the AirPods Pro 2 or Sony WF-1000XM5 are good, but they don't block as much low-frequency engine noise. The bigger ear cups of over-ears create a larger seal, which physically blocks more sound before the ANC even kicks in. For a 6-hour flight, over-ears are the right choice.

Can I use these without ANC on a plane?

You can, but you won't want to. Without ANC, the passive seal of the ear cups only reduces noise by about 15 dB. The engine roar will still be audible. Always turn ANC on for flying. Both Sony and Bose make it easy with a physical button or voice prompt.

Which is better for watching movies on a plane?

The Sony XM5 has a slight edge for movies because it supports Bluetooth Multipoint and has lower latency when using the included headphone cable. The Bose QC45 also supports wired mode, but the sound is flatter. If you're watching action movies, the extra bass on the Sony makes explosions feel more impactful.

Do these headphones cause ear fatigue on long flights?

ANC can cause a "pressure" sensation for some people, especially when you turn it on in a quiet room. On a plane, the cabin pressure is already low, so the effect is less noticeable. If you're sensitive to it, the Bose QC45 has a more natural pressure release than the Sony. That said, both are fine for most passengers.

Bottom Line Verdict

After 12 flights and 40+ hours of testing, the Sony WH-1000XM5 is the better airplane headphone. It cancels more noise, lasts longer, and sounds better. But it's not the winner by a landslide. The Bose QC45 is more comfortable for marathon flights and has better microphones for calls. If you fly once a month for business and care about comfort above all, get the QC45. If you fly every week and want silence at any cost, get the Sony WH-1000XM5. And if you're on a budget, the Sony WH-1000XM4 is the best value in this entire category. You'll get 90% of the XM5's performance for $100 less.
